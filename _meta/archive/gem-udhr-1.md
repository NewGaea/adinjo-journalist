This has been a fascinating linguistic excavation. The structure of **Qonjo** reveals a language that is highly logical, agglutinative, and deeply philosophical, prioritizing harmony ("right-wishing") and collective relations ("everyone-to-everyone").

Here is the comprehensive grammatical summary of the Qonjo language as established by our analysis of this text.

### 1. Phonology

**Script & Sounds:**

- **Vowels:** Standard 5-vowel system ($a, e, i, o, u$) plus **y** (/y/).
    
    - **Diacritics:** Acute ($é$) and Grave ($è$) mark stress and vowel quality (tense vs. lax).
        
- **Consonants:**
    
    - **j** = /ʒ/ (Voiced postalveolar fricative, as in "measure").
        
    - **c** = /ts/ (Voiceless alveolar affricate, as in "cats").
        
    - **r** = Allophonic. Tap [ɾ] between vowels; Approximant [ɹ] elsewhere.
        
    - **sh / ch** = /ʃ/ and /tʃ/.
        
- **Phonotactics:**
    
    - Allows complex codas ($rist, -lm$) and clusters.
        
    - Tendency toward open syllables in roots ($ba, dy, ga-ya$).
        

---

### 2. Morphology

Qonjo is agglutinative, building meaning through predictable suffix stacks and sophisticated infixing.

**Nominal Morphology:**

- **Plural Number:** **-nu** (_dyn_ "person" $\rightarrow$ _dynu_; _ba_ "this" $\rightarrow$ _banu_).
    
- **Derivational Prefixes:**
    
    - **lan-a-:** Reciprocal Universal.
        
    - _dyn_ (person) $\rightarrow$ _adin_ (all people) $\rightarrow$ _lanadin_ ("everyone to everyone else").
        
- **Attributive:** **-ma** or **-uma** (_nahertu_ "nature" $\rightarrow$ _nahertuma_ "of nature/natural").
    
- **Compounding:** _rist_ (right/true) + _kirénos_ (wishing) $\rightarrow$ _rist-kirénos_ ("conscience").
    

**Verbal Morphology:**

- **Stative Form:** **-au**. Turns a verb into an adjective/participle describing a state.
    
    - _ijin_ (know) $\rightarrow$ _ijinau_ ("knowing" or "wise").
        
    - _livr_ (free) $\rightarrow$ _livrau_ ("free/unbound").
        
- **Tense:**
    
    - **Past:** **-tot**.
        
    - **Future:** **-tochi**.
        
- **Voice (The Infix Rule):**
    
    - The Passive Voice marker **-lo-** is infixed **into the tense suffix**.
        
    - Past Passive: _-tot_ + _-lo-_ $\rightarrow$ **-tolot**.
        
    - Future Passive: _-tochi_ + _-lo-_ $\rightarrow$ **-tolochi**.
        
- **Mood:**
    
    - **Permissive/Obligative:** **-galm**.
        
    - _Logic:_ "Future Passive Permissive" ("It will be permitted to be done") functions as a moral imperative ("Should/Must").
        

---

### 3. Syntax

**Sentence Structure:**

- **Primary Order:** **Topic - Subject - Object - Verb**.
    
    - _Kami_ (Topic) … _gayatolot_ (Verb).
        
- **Topic Marker:** _Kami_ ("Among…" / "Regarding…").
    
- **Conjunctions:**
    
    - **hi:** "And" (Union).
        
    - **bakira:** "Therefore" (Logical progression).
        
- **Particles:**
    
    - **i:** "Of" (Genitive/Relational).
        
    - **ni:** "In" (Locative/Instrumental).

### 5. Final Translation

**The Text:**

> _"Kami dynu ijinau, kuri banu livrau hi orom lakulu i nahertuma gayatolot. Shinu chijin hi rist-kirénos bancolot, bakira lanadin ni lav i andévo hi ancoté kejastolochigalm."_

**The Grammatical Decomposition:**

> **Kami** (TOPIC) **dyn-nu** (person-PL) **ijin-au** (know-STAT), **kuri** (ALL) **ba-nu** (this-PL) **livr-au** (free-STAT) **hi** (AND) **orom** (equal) **lakulu** (law) **i** (OF) **nahertu-ma** (nature-ATTR) **gaya-to-lo-t** (life-PAST-PASS-PAST).
> 
> **Shinu** (THEY) **chijin** (reason) **hi** (AND) **rist-kirénos** (conscience) **banc-to-lo-t** (grant-PAST-PASS-PAST), **bakira** (THEREFORE) **lanadin** (everyone-to-everyone) **ni** (IN) **lav** (spirit) **i** (OF) **andévo** (brotherhood) **hi** (AND) **ancoté** (sisterhood) **kejas-to-lo-chi-galm** (honor-FUT-PASS-FUT-PERM).

**The English Translation:**

> "Among the thinking peoples, all these by the law of nature are born free and equal. They are endowed with reason and conscience; therefore, they should honor one another in the spirit of brotherhood and sisterhood."
