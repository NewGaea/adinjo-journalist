## 06. June

### 19 June
#### Final Fantasy I Class Names

I had also translated the class names

> [!Note] Adinjo Text from 2003
> 
> - [Dorjakhe](lexicon/d/dorjaxe.md) - Adventurer, Warrior, Soldier
>   - from 'dorja' meaning 'adventurer', this is one who lives off of adventures rather than a normal set of skills and/or job.
> - [Nashikh](lexicon/n/nashix.md) - Thief, Rogue
>   - from 'nashiru' meaning 'to steal', this is any person who uses theft, swindling, or cons to gain their money. The Nashikh is sort of a Robin Hood-esque character though, as the typical portrayal is of a noble nashikh, stealing to right some wrong, or for some noble cause.
> - [Ahime](lexicon/a/ahime.md) - Itinerant, Monk, Martial Artist
>   - from 'ahi' and 'jime' meaning 'travel' and 'martial arts', respectively. A Journeyman Maertial Artist, generally seeking to aid the less fortunate. Ahiménu are often portrayed alongside of Nashikhu in stories, as both have a noble cause at heart.
> - [Zhedai](lexicon/j/jedai.md) - Spellblade, Battle Mage
>   - from 'ze' and 'dai', meaning 'sage' and 'body'. A general warrior sage, typically denoting any "multi-class" magic-user/priest with warrior type skills. Zhedai'u often band together with Ahimenu or other types of adventurers, lending their knowledge of magic and skill with the sword to help balance a team.
> - [Kharon](lexicon/x/xáron.md) - Wizard, Mage
>   - literally 'magic-user' or 'mage', often referring to elemental and conjuring magics, or 'dark arts'. Kharon'taku, or student mages, are rarely let to leave their studies, and so only a graduated Kharon is likely to be met traveling.
> - [Koenekh](lexicon/k/koenex.md) - Priest, Cleric
>   - literally derived from koen, which means 'priest' these are students, often those other professions would have in the "journeyman" phase. These young priests are allowed to roam, but expected not to forsake their duties to whatever deity they serve (after all, not all Koenekhu are actually Adin).

### 26 June

#### Final Fantasy I Prologue

> [!Note] Adinjo Text from 2003
> 
> Kushi muri almarima walu deirinu.
> Romu folren, ch'honu eldharen, hi sug jalu ich daharen.
> Sug adin ebron, kushi nentir ni dan eloshin:
> "Monsi walu almarin sug muri, lukhan dorja tashemle ventrotoci……"
> Goju doretan, lukhan dorja Kornelia hemle ventrotoci.

```ngloss
\ex Kushi muri almarima walu deirinu
\gl kushi [ku.shi] [GEN-3PL] [their]
    muri [muri] [world] [world]
    almarima* [almar.i.ma] [rule.?*.ATTRIB] [ruled by]
    walu [walu] [evil] [evil]
    deirinu [deirin.u] [power.PL] [powers]
\ft Their world is ruled by evil powers.
```

```ngloss
\ex Romu folren, ch'honu eldharen, hi sug jalu ich daharen.
\ft The winds stop, the seas rage, and the earth is rotting.
```

```ngloss
\ex Sug adin ebron, kushi nentir ni dan eloshin:
\ft The people wait, their hope in a prophecy:
```

```ngloss
\ex Monsi walu almarin sug muri, lukhan dorja tas'hemlé ventrotoci……
\ft When evil rules the world, four warriors will come forward……
```

```ngloss
\ex Goju doretan, lukhan dorja KORNELIA hemlé ventrotochi.
\ft After a long journey, four warriors approach Cornelia.
```
