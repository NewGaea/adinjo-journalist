Modern Adinjo is the immediate precursor to the formalization of Adinjo Journalist, and is highly similar to Journalist. It is less rigidly structured, and the biggest difference is that it does not have any loan words from after roughly the time of Classical Rome and Julius Caesar -- Latin or earlier are the only valid sources of borrowings.

Phonologically and phonotactically, it is identical to Journalist. The biggest changes are that its writing is slightly more etymological, and that it will freely borrow words phonetically so long as they don't conflict too greatly with existing familiar words.

## Ease of Translation

As long as a word is not borrowed from Atrusius or the Modern World, the Adinjo Journalist lexicon is compatible with it.
