# Reference Grammar for "Space Latin"

Here is the comprehensive **Reference Grammar and Lexicon (v1.0)** for the project "Space Latin" (Samples 1–17).

This document summarizes all confirmed rules, vocabulary, and narrative context established to date. It is designed to serve as a "Clean Slate" foundation for future translation work.

---

### **I. Syntax & Sentence Structure**

**1. Word Order**

- **Basic Order:** **SOV** (Subject - Object - Verb).
    
    - _Ex:_ `Yi fi ijin.` ("I her see." => "I see her.")
        
- **Adpositions:** **Postpositional**.
    
    - _Ex:_ `Romurén nikar` ("Shuttle inside" => "inside (a/the) shuttle").
        
    - _Ex:_ `Natha hemlé` ("Here towards" => "come here" or "arrive here").
        

**2. Noun Phrases**

- **Adjectives:** Follow the Noun (**Head-Initial**).
    
    - _Ex:_ `Mokuretaxu dama` ("Recruits new" => "new recruits").
        
- **Quantifiers/Numbers:** Precede the Noun (**Head-Final**).
    
    - _Ex:_ `Larn mokuretaxu` ("Two recruits").
        
- **Genitive/Possessive:** Marked by prefix **`Ku-`** on the possessor or possessed object.
    
    - _Ex:_ `Ku-Bébí sinju` ("Baby's face").
        

**3. Interrogatives**

- **`Aur`**: General Question Particle (begins the sentence or clause of the question).
    
- **`Techi`**: May be used for a Tag Question ("Correct?" / "Right?") used at the end of a statement.
    
- **Wh-Words:** `Sif` (Why), `Monsi` (When), `Ira` (Which/Selection).

### **II. Morphology**

#### **1. The Verb System**

The language splits verbs into two primary conjugation classes.

| **Tense**             | **Class I (Vowel Shift)**         | **Class II (S-Form/Infix)**  |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Examples**          | _Tak_ (Leave), _Hemlé_ (Approach) | _Kempo_ (Do), _Folre_ (Stop) |
| **Present (+) / (-)** | **`-ton` / `-tona`**              | **`-s` / `-yas`**            |
| **Past (+) / (-)**    | **`-tot` / `-tota`**              | **`-ns` / `-yans`**          |
| **Future (+) / (-)**  | **`-tochi` / `-tocha`**           | **`-ykun` / `-yakun`**       |

**Other Verbal Features:**

- **Passive Voice:** Suffix **`-ol`** (e.g., `Biderol` = "Is opened").
    
- **Phonological Rules:**
    
    - **Cluster Reduction:** `/rt/` $\to$ `/ɾ/` (Tap). (_Mar_ + _ton_ $\to$ `Maron`).
        
    - **Hardening:** Voiced affricates devoice/harden before voiceless suffixes. (_Rez_ $\to$ `Recz`, _Cij_ $\to$ `Cidj`).
        

#### **2. Nouns & Pronouns**

- **Plural:** Suffix (words enging in vowels) **`-nu`**. (_Sinju_ $\to$ `Sinju-nu`). Suffix (words ending in consonants) **`-u`**. (_Kieres_ $\to$ `Kieres-u`).
    
- **Pronoun Grid:**
    

| **Person**   | **Singular** | **Plural**         | **Dual/Special**                                      |
| ------------ | ------------ | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **1st**      | **Yi** (I)   | **Ki** (We)        | --                                                    |
| **2nd**      | **Ci** (You) | **Cinu** (You all) | **Cilarn** (Both of you)                              |
| **3rd Masc** | **Ji**       | --                 | --                                                    |
| **3rd Fem**  | **Fi**       | --                 | --                                                    |
| **3rd Neut** | **Xi**       | **Shi**            | --                                                    |
| **Neg/Null** | --           | --                 | (?) **Dekcin** (Neither/None; unconfirmed hypothesis) |

#### **3. Derivation & Contraction**

- **Universal Prefix `A-`:**
    
    - `Aye` (Hand) $\to$ `Áye` (Crew/Troops - "All Hands").
        
    - `Yuga` (Thing?) $\to$ `Ayuga` (Everything).
        
- **Genitive Elision:** `Ku-` drops the `/u/` before a vowel.
    
    - `Ku` + `Áye` $\to$ `K'áye` ("Of the Crew").

### **III. The Lexicon**

#### **Nouns**

- **`Áye`**: Crew / Troops / Hands.
    
- **`Bitke`**: Hatch / Door / Lock.
    
- **`Demargel`**: Protocol / Command / Standing Orders.
    
- **`Felimin`**: Inspection (Process).
    
- **`Mokuretaxu`**: Recruits.
    
- **`Romurén`**: Shuttle.
    
- **`Saukentax`**: Commander.
    
- **`Sinju`**: Face.
    
- **`Tok`**: Moment / Minute.
    
- **`Yanis'dun`**: Whisper / Rumor (Diminutive).
    

#### **Verbs**

- **`Bider`**: To open.
    
- **`Cij`**: To lock / close.
    
- **`Folre`**: To stop.
    
- **`Hemlé`**: To approach.
    
- **`Ijin`**: To see / meet.
    
- **`Jimar`**: To hear.
    
- **`Kannu`**: To refuse.
    
- **`Kempo`**: To do / handle.
    
- **`Lorn`**: To flatten (face) / make unexpressive.
    
- **`Mar`**: To walk.
    
- **`Promei`**: To log / record.
    
- **`Recz` / `Rez`**: To stand / present oneself.
    
- **`Tak`**: To move away.
    
- **`Yani`**: To speak.
    
- **`Zen`**: To inspect / look at.
    

#### **Adjectives, Numbers & Modifiers**

- **`Ankoro`**: Existing / Present.
    
- **`Anpera`**: Pleased / Content.
    
- **`Dama`**: New.
    
- **`Hort`**: Scary / Terrifying.
    
- **`Larn`**: **Two** (Cardinal).
    
    - _`Larnirin`_: **Second** (Ordinal) / "Second Class" (Failing).
        
- **`Win`**: **One** (Cardinal - Reconstructed).
    
    - _`Winirin`_: **First** (Ordinal) / "First Class" (Passing).
        
- **`Monsi`** (?): Alone / Solo (Contextual hypothesis).
    

#### **Particles & Time**

- **`Ba`**: This (Proximate Demonstrative).
    
- **`Jia`**: Day.
    
- **`Bajia`**: Today ("This Day").
    
- **`Hi`**: And (Subject).
    
- **`I`**: Of (Partitive/Possessive).

#### The "Inspection" Incident

Two new recruits, Arìk (nervous, prone to complaining) and Bébí (competent, star pupil), arrive on a shuttle. Before they can exit, the shuttle is locked by Bébí, who has just seen a face that scared her. Arìk panics because the inspecting officer is Commander Cheyrin, a terrifying figure known to the troops (áye) by rumors (yanis'dun).

The recruits are ordered to unlock the hatches (`bitke`). Arìk tries to settle Bébí and remind her to stand on proper protocol. Cheyrin interrogates them about the locked door.

**The Current State:**

- **Bébí** admits she arrived on site "today" (`bajia`). **Commander Cheyrin** observes that it is only her  **first day** (`jia winirin`).
    
- **Arìk** admits he arrived "yesterday" (`jia kérin`, `kérinjia`). **Commander Cheyrin** observes that it is his **second day** (`jia larinirin`) .
