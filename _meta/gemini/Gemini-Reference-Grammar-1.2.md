# Reference Grammar for "Space Latin"

Here is the comprehensive **Reference Grammar and Lexicon (v1.0)** for the project "Space Latin" (Samples 1–17).

This document summarizes all confirmed rules, vocabulary, and narrative context established to date. It is designed to serve as a "Clean Slate" foundation for future translation work.

---
## 1. Syntax & Sentence Structure

### 1.1 Word Order

- **Basic Order:** **SOV** (Subject - Object - Verb).
	- _Ex:_ `Yi fi baciton.` ("I her see." => "I see her.")
- **Equative (Copula):** **S P Copula** (Subject - Predicate - `ic`).
	- _Ex:_ `Yi BÉBÍ ic.` ("I Baby am" $\to$ "I am Bébí").
- **Adpositions:** **Postpositional**.
	- _Ex:_ `Romurén nikar` ("Shuttle inside" => "inside (a/the) shuttle").
	- _Ex:_ `Natha hemlé` ("Here towards" => "come here" or "arrive here").

### 1.2. Noun Phrases

- **Adjectives:** Follow the Noun (**Head-Initial**).
	- _Ex:_ `Mokuretaxu dama` ("Recruits new" => "new recruits").
- **Quantifiers/Numbers:** Precede the Noun (**Head-Final**).
    - _Ex:_ `Larn mokuretaxu` ("Two recruits").
- **Genitive/Possessive:** Marked by prefix **`Ku-`** on the possessor or possessed object.
	- _Ex:_ `Ku-Bébí sinju` ("Baby's face").

**3. Discourse & Particles**

- **`Aur`**: General Question Marker (Scope: Sentence or Clause, initial).
- **`Lo`**: Topic Marker / Focus ("To this point/time...", "Regarding…", "Look here…"). 
- **`Techi`**: Affirmation / Tag Question ("Correct", "Right", "Yes").
- **`Ma`**: But / Yet (Conjunction).
- **`Ka`**: If (Conditional).
- **`Hi`**: And (Noun conjunction).
- **Wh-Words:** `Sif` (Why), `Monsi` (When), `Ira` (Which/Selection). Only form explicit questions when accompanied by `aur`.
    
## **2. Morphology**

### 2.1. The Verb System

The language splits verbs into two primary conjugation classes.

#### 2.1.1 Class I (The T-Stems)

Characterized by /t/ infixation.

Examples: `Tak` (to move away), `Hemlé` (to move towards, to approach), `Baci` (to see).

| **Tense / Mood**     | **Positive** | **Negative**   | **Note**                         |
| -------------------- | ------------ | -------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Present**          | **`-ton`**   | **`-tona`**    | Current, specific action.        |
| **Past**             | **`-tot`**   | **`-tota`**    | Completed action.                |
| **Future / Imp**     | **`-tochi`** | **`-tocha`**   | Future event OR **Command**.     |
| **Gnomic / Passive** | **`-tol`**   | **`-tola`**(?) | Habitual truth OR Passive voice. |

#### 2.1.2. Class II (The S-Stems)

Characterized by /s/ or /k/ morphology. Voice Undifferentiated (No Passive form exists).

Examples: `Kempo` (to do), `Folre` (to stop).

| **Tense / Mood** | **Positive** | **Negative** | **Note**                        |
| ---------------- | ------------ | ------------ | ------------------------------- |
| **Present**      | **`-s`**     | **`-yas`**   | Vowel/Consonant stem dependent? |
| **Past**         | **`-ns`**    | **`-yans`**  |                                 |
| **Future / Imp** | **`-ykun`**  | **`-yakun`** | Future event OR **Command**.    |
#### 2.1.3. The Copula

- **Verb:** **`ic`** 
	- Appears invariant for Person/Number in current data.
	- Is reported to be a Class II verb that breaks certain conventions of Class II either because it is the copula or because it is only one syllable. Needs further investigation if we find other words that follow this paradigm?

#### 2.1.4. Phonological Rules
- **Phonological Rules:**
	- **Cluster Reduction:** `/rt/` $\to$ `/ɾ/` (Tap). (_Mar_ + _ton_ $\to$ `Maron`).
	- **Hardening:** Voiced affricates devoice/harden before voiceless suffixes. (_Rez_ $\to$ `Recz`, _Cij_ $\to$ `Cidj`).
	- These may suggest that other sounds close to \<t> experience similar reductions or mutation? We may wish to question our informants about this in more detail

#### 2.2. Nouns & Pronouns

#### 2.2.1. Number
- **Plural:**
	- Suffix (words ending in vowels) **`-nu`**. (_Sinju_ $\to$ `Sinju-nu`).
	- Suffix (words ending in consonants) **`-u`**. (_Kieres_ $\to$ `Kieres-u`).
- **Universal:**
	- Prefix (`a-`), appears to refer to mass or collective numbers of things.
	- Does not require normal plural marking.
		- `Aye` (Hand) $\to$ `Áye` (Crew/Troops - "All Hands").
			- Suggests a mutation where long \<a> becomes \<á>(?)
		- `Yuga` (Thing?) $\to$ `Ayuga` (Everything).

#### 2.2.2 **Pronouns**

| **Person**   | **Singular** | Singular Genitive | **Plural**         | Plural Genitive | **Dual/Special**         | Dual/Special Genitive            |
| ------------ | ------------ | ----------------- | ------------------ | --------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| **1st**      | **Yi** (I)   | **Kuyi** (My)     | **Ki** (We)        | Kuki            | --                       |                                  |
| **2nd**      | **Ci** (You) | **Kuci** (Your)   | **Cinu** (You all) | Kucinu          | **Cilarn** (Both of you) | **Kucilarn** (both of yours) (?) |
| **3rd Masc** | **Ji** (He)  | **Kuji** (Boys')  | --                 |                 | --                       |                                  |
| **3rd Fem**  | **Fi** (She) | **Kufi** (Girls') | --                 |                 | --                       |                                  |

#### **3. Derivation & Contraction**

- **Genitive Elision:** `Ku-` drops the `/u/` before a vowel.
	- `Ku` + `Áye` $\to$ `K'áye` ("Of the Crew").

### 2.3 General Morphological Affixes & Enclitics

- **Plural:** **`-nu`** (Vowel-final), **`-u`** (Consonant-final).
- **Suffix Modifiers:**
    - **`-'dul`**: **Augmentative** (big, large, much).
    - **`-'dun`**: **Diminutive** (small, less, little)
    - **`-'rin`**: **Positive Quality** (good, well, fine).

#### The "Inspection" Incident

Before they can exit, the shuttle is locked by Bébí, who has just seen a face that scared her. Arìk panics because the inspecting officer is Commander Cheyrin, a terrifying figure known to the troops (áye) by rumors (yanis'dun).

The recruits are ordered to unlock the hatches (`bitke`). Arìk tries to settle Bébí and remind her to stand on proper protocol. Cheyrin interrogates them about the locked door.

**The Current State:**

- **Bébí** admits she arrived on site "today" (`bajia`). **Commander Cheyrin** observes that it is only her  **first day** (`jia winirin`).
    
- **Arìk** admits he arrived "yesterday" (`jia kérin`, `kérinjia`). **Commander Cheyrin** observes that it is his **second day** (`jia larinirin`) .
