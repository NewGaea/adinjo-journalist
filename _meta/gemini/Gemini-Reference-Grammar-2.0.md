## **I. Phonology & Lexical Foundations**

- **\[Assumption] The Gerund/Abstract Suffix (`-s`):** The suffix `-s` transforms a verb root into a noun or gerund (e.g., _Yani_ "to say" $\rightarrow$ _Yanis_ "saying/speaking").
- **\[Standard] Diminutive/Qualitative Suffixes (`-dun`, `-dul`):** 
    - **`-dun` \[Standard]:** "Little" or "Small." When applied to verbs, it indicates low volume or intensity (_Yanitoldun_ - "is whispered"). 
    - **`-dul` \[Standard]:** "Great" or "Intense." Used to amplify the scale or emotional weight of a noun or verb (_Lamatot-'dul_ - "intensely terrified").
- **Pluralization (`-nu`):** Nouns are pluralized by the suffix `-nu` (_Bitke_ "door" $\rightarrow$ _Bitkenu_ "doors"). 

- **Phonological Rules:**
	- **Cluster Reduction:** `/rt/` $\to$ `/ɾ/` (Tap). (_Mar_ + _ton_ $\to$ `Maron`).
	- **Hardening:** Voiced affricates devoice/harden before voiceless suffixes. (_Rez_ $\to$ `Recz`, _Cij_ $\to$ `Cidj`).
	- These may suggest that other sounds close to \<t> experience similar reductions or mutation? We may wish to question our informants about this in more detail

## **II. Verb Paradigms: The Class System**

Space Latin divides verbs into two primary classes based on the nature of the action.

#### **Class I**

\[Assumption] Actions involving the material world or basic bodily functions (e.g., _Bider_ "to open," _Kruchi_ "to eat," _Mar_ "to walk").

- **Present:** `-ton`
    
- **Future Negative:** **`-tocha`**
    
- **Passive Past:** **`-tolot`**
    
- **Passive/Habitual State:** **`-tol`**
    

#### **Class II: Stative & Administrative Actions**

Actions involving states of being, logic, or administrative status (e.g., _Ic_ "to be," _Folr_ "to stop").

- **Present:** `Ic` (Unique irregular root)
    
- **Conditional Negative:** **`-yakun`**
    

> **[Assumption]:** We have assumed the `-yakun` ending is exclusive to Class II for conditional logic, though it may also serve as a general "uncertainty" marker in wider contexts.

### **III. Morphosyntactic Assembly**

The "Assembly Rule" is the most vital structural finding of this session:

Root + [Tense/Voice Suffix] + [Qualitative Suffix]

- _Correct:_ **`Yanitoldun`** (Say + Passive/Habitual + Small) = "Is habitually whispered."
    
- _Incorrect:_ `Yanis'dunol` (The gerund cannot accept tense/voice markers).

### **IV. The Logic of Conjunctions [Contextual]**

The choice of conjunction defines the "Logical Vector" of a sentence.

- **`Ma` (So/Thus):** Indicates a "Chain of Liability." If State A exists, State B follows inevitably.
    
- **`Kend` (But):** Indicates "Contraindication." Attempts to decouple the speaker from the consequence.
    
- **`Hi` (Union):** Simply links two existing states into a single administrative context.
    

---

### **V. The System of Authority [High-Complexity]**

#### **1. Zero-Anaphora (Pro-Drop)**

In Space Latin, pronouns are "clutter." Once a subject is established (either by a noun or the context of the room), it is deleted.

- **[Contextual]:** This is particularly common in military or high-stakes environments where efficiency is prioritized over clarity for the subordinate.
    

#### **2. The `Ku-` Prefix & `i` Particle**

- **`i` [Standard]:** Standard possessive "of." Used between two nouns (_Dekchinu i áye_ - "Words of the crew").
    
- **`Ku-` [Assumption/Contextual]:** A possessive prefix for Proper Nouns. It "anchors" the subsequent logic to that individual’s record (_KuBÉBÍ_ - "Belonging to Bébí").
    

#### **3. The Epithet System [Contextual]**

Individuals are often replaced by temporal or administrative data points.

- **`Jia` + Ordinal:** "Day-State" (_Jia Winirin_ - First Day).
    
- **`Aurok` + Ordinal:** "Matter-State" (_Aurok Winirin_ - First Matter).
    
- **[Assumption]:** We assume this metonymy is used to dehumanize or "log" subjects within a formal record.

### **VI. Conditional Framing**

A conditional sentence is "bracketed" by two markers:

1. **`Ka`** (The "If" Prime): Places the listener in a state of uncertainty.
    
2. **`-yakun` / `-tocha`**: The verb suffix that closes the logical loop.
