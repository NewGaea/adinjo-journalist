# Gemini's Text Corpus for "Space Latin"

Here is the complete archive of the corpus to date, with translations updated to reflect the most current grammatical and lexical understanding (v1.0).

### Part I: Original Corpus

#### **Sample 1**

> Trini shoxaton.
> 
> "A bird is singing."

> Trini shoxatol.
> 
> "Sings the bird."

> Trininu shoxaton.
> 
> "Birds are singing."

> Trininu shoxatol.
> 
> "Sing the birds."

_I am told that these forms are the singular and plural nouns, marked by adding `-nu` to the root, and the simple present tense `-ton` and present passive voice `-tol`. I do not understand what it means exactly to use a passive voice in this context, but I am assured it is valid in Space Latin._

#### Sample 2

> Gent ayginston.
> 
> "A child is playing."

> Gent ayginstol.
> 
> "Plays a child."

> Gentu ayginston.
> 
> "Children are playing."

> Gentu ayginstol.
> 
> "Play children."

_I questioned my sources for a time, and came to recognize that this use of the passive `-tol` is a form of Gnomic statement, and also appears to be used to describe a habitual action. I realized this some time after they began saying of me `Ji aurentol.` which seems to mean that they perceived of me that inquiry was in my nature._

#### Sample 3

> Kita auf yaniton.
> 
> "A dog is barking." (Lit: "Dog 'woof' says.")

> Kitanu auf yanitol.
> 
> "Dogs bark." (Lit: "Dog.PL 'woof' say.PASS)

_It's fascinating. They do not have a word for the manner in which a dog expresses its voice, but a sound that imitate it, the `auf`, and they desribe how a dog speaks by saying that a dog "says" `auf`._

#### Sample 4

> Kieres mathi zum yaniton.
> 
> "A honeybee is buzzing." (Lit: "Builder honey 'bzzz' says.")

> Kieresu mathi zum yanitol.
> 
> "Honeybees buzz." (Lit: "Builder honey 'bzzz' says.")

_It appears that certain animals use phrasal names. For example, the humble "honeybee" (indeed any "bee" in English) is the phrase `kieres mathi` which I am told means something like the honey carpenter or the honey builder, for these bees build artificial shelters in a manner similar to how men build homes._

#### Sample 5

> BÉBÍ géxatot.
> 
> "Baby laughed."

> BÉBÍ géxatolot.
> 
> "Baby used to laugh." or "Baby laughed (involuntarily)."

_It appears the convention in Space Latin is to use an ALL-CAPS format when presenting proper nouns, or at least proper names. My source confirmed that the name Bébí is phonologically analogous to the English word "baby," though they were unable to provide a meaning behind the name._

#### Sample 6

> Kuha ellores.
> 
> "A sun shines."


> Sug kuha ellores.
> 
> "The sun shines."

_This sample introduced my first exposure to Class II verbs, and to the definite article, `sug`, though it seems that this article is used only rarely, apparently when ti is necessary to draw great attention to a noun. I have seen very few other examples of the use of `sug` to date. It is also worth noting that `kuha` is not a word for star, but for a star that lights the sky by day, or a star around which worlds live, so it seems to well fit the sense of a "sun."_

#### Sample 7

> Romlan brauton.
> 
> "A wind is blowing (itself)."
> 
> Romulan brautol.
> 
> "The wind blows (itself)."

_I once misread and misheard this suffix, `-lan` as `-lin`, which appears to have made me appear a bit foolish and flighty. While I am still uncertain what it is that `-lin` means, `-lan` serves to mark a noun in a reflexive case, making the noun acts upon itself with the verb. _

#### Sample 8

> Yi kita baciton.
> 
> "I see a dog."

> Kita yi bacitol.
> 
> "I'm seen by a dog."

_I've not learned the words yet for that famous quote about the difference between mundanity and novelty, but it does seem that I could at least construct it, were I to learn the word meaning "to bite."_

#### Sample 9

> Ci agro hemlé marton.
> 
> "I am walking to the market."

> Ci agro hemlé martol.
> 
> "I am walked to the market."

_It appears that the structure of a sentence may be quite rigid, but there is an elegance to the simple passive inflection (and its versatility) in Space Latin. Devoid of context we only know that both of these discuss the present, and that former is a more agentive "walk" than the latter, which makes the interlocutor less volitional. Perhaps this flexibility is a source of nuance and complexity where English might be clearer but require more words?_

#### Sample 10

> Aur ci trini baciton
> 
> "So, you see the bird?"

> Techi, yi trini baciton.
> 
> "Yes, I see the bird."

> Aur ci kitanu jimarton
> 
> "Do you hear the dogs?"

> Ha, yi atakama jimartona.
> 
> "No, I hear nothing at all."

_`Aur` is apparently a verbal question or indeterminacy indicator. It often appears at the start of a sentene to indicate a question, though it can also precede clauses or phrases that are in some manner uncertain._

>

#### **Sample 17**

> Yi folrens.
> 
> "I stopped."

#### **Sample 18**

> Aur ci folres?
> 
> "Do you stop?" or "Are you stopping?"

#### **Sample 19**

> Kucilan folres, \[kucilan] ijinton.
> 
> "Stop yourself, know yourself."

_This is an insteresting observation, apparently by chaining several conjugated verbs, a sentence (at least one using the reflexive structure) may form parallel statements with only added verbs. Theseverbs only need to be conjugated, they do not need to share the same class._

#### **Sample 20**

> Yi folrens, ma ci hemléton.
> 
> "I stopped, yet you are approaching."

### **Part III: Military Recruits**

_The "Inspection Arc" featuring Commander **Cheyrin**, Ensign **Darmi**, and Recruits **Arìk** (Arick) and **Bébí** (Baby)._

#### Sample 21

Context: Bébí introduces herself to another person, Arìk. 

> BÉBÍ: Hayl, yi BÉBÍ ic.
> "Baby: Hello, I am Baby."

> ARÌK: Hayl BÉBI. Yi ARÌK menatol.
> "Arick: Hello Baby. I am called Arick."

> BÉBÍ: Aa, Hayl ARÌK. Aur ci natha hemléton dama jua?
> Baby: "O, hello Arick. So, are you also newly arrived?"

> ARÌK: Techi, a jia kérin yi natha hemlétot.
> "Arick: Yes, um, I arrived here a day before (now)."

Context: Commander Cheyrin mistakes Ensign Darmi for a new arrival in the dim light.

#### Sample 22

Context: Cheyrin reports the status of the recruit shuttle.

> CHEYRIN: Dandin DARMI, lo aur delman mokuretaxu dama natha hemlétot?
> "Cheyrin: Ensign Darmi, status: About the new recruits, are they here?"

> DARMI: Daiti, kushi romurén, xi ju hemlé folrens.
> "Darmi: Ma'am, their shuttle, it just now stopped."

> CHEYRIN: Tech, yi hemlé Dandin.
> "Cheyrin: Confirmed, come to me Ensign."

#### Sample 23

Context: Panic inside the shuttle. Bébí is laughing from terror at the fact she's just closed the shuttle's bay doors in the face of some officer.

> BÉBÍ: Kufi sinju yi lamatot-'dul sugo!
> 
> "Her face really scared me badly!"

> ARÌK: Folres, folres; ka folryakun, ci fi jimartolochi…
> 
> "Stop it, stop it; if you don't stop, you will be heard by her…"

> BÉBÍ: Aur fi kempo-mer? Lo, ki romurén nikar ic.
> 
> "Can she do that? Right now, we are (still) inside the shuttle."

> ARÌK: Shi fi jimarmer ayuga yanitol!
> 
> "Shh! They say she can hear everything!"
#### Sample 24

Context: The shuttle's bay doors have just been closed in the face of Commander Cheyrin, and she is taken aback and questioning.

> CHEYRIN: Aur sif shi bitke kempo-ns… Dandin, Shi natha hemlé romurén tak-tochi! Ju!
> 
> "Who did \[oeprated] the hatches? …Ensign, Extract them from the shuttle! Now!"

> DARMI: Mokuretaxu, kuci bitke i romurén bider-ochi ju. Ba demargel ic, Saukentax CHEYRIN inda.
> 
> "Recruits, open your hatches of the shuttle now. This is an order, from Commander Cheyrin."

Context: Inside the shuttle, the message from Ensign Darmi shakes Arìk and Bébí

> ARÌK: Cholrex! Kuyi jia larnirin lamo! …Ki… cilarn demargel i Saukentax kannu-tocha.
> 
> "Crap! Only my second day! …We… both of us… cannot refuse Orders of the Commander."
> 
> ARÌK: Kuci sinju, lorn kas-ykun!
> 
> "Make your face flat \[poker-faced]!"
> 
> BÉBÍ: Aa! Tech, yi kemp-ykun-'rin. Yi fi Saukentax ankoro ijin-tota!
> 
> "Ah! Yes, I will do so. I never knew the Commander would exist \[meet us]!"

#### Sample 25

Context: The hatches open. The recruits try to compose themselves as the officers board.

> NARRATOR: Bitkenu i romurén bider-ol. ARÌK maron'dun, BÉBÍ kérin recz-ol.
> 
> "The locks of the shuttle are opened. Arìk shuffles [in the back], Bébí stands [at attention] at the front."
> 
> NARRATOR: KuBÉBÍ sinju lorn ic, fi yani-tona. DARMI hi CHEYRIN shi larn mokuretaxu zen-ton, kushi sinjunu lorn ic jua.
> 
> "Bébí's face is flat, she does not speak. Darmi and Cheyrin inspect them, the two recruits, and their faces are flat also."

#### Sample 26

Context: Cheyrin interrogates the recruits about the locked hatch.

> CHEYRIN: Mokuretaxu, reczochi!
> 
> "Recruits, Attention!"
> 
> NARRATOR: ARÌK hi BÉBÍ reczon'dul ju.
> 
> "Arìk and Bébí stand tall (come to attention) immediately."
> 
> CHEYRIN: Yi ci hemlé anpera icha.
> 
> "I am not pleased with you."
> 
> CHEYRIN: Sif aur cinu bitke-nu cidj-ot? Hi, ira larn ci kempo-ns, hi ira ci kemp-yans?
> 
> "Why did you all lock the hatches? And, which of the two of you did (locked it), and which of you did not ()?"

_The question feels like "Of you two, which of you was involved in closing the doors, and which of you wasn't?" It feels as if she's offering them an opportunity to deny any wrongdoing before they get off on the wrong foot, without assuming them to be innocent, and perhaps expecting her presence to intimidate them into honesty all the same. There may still be room to improve this passage, at least for the English translation._

#### Sample 27

Context: The interrogation presses on.

> NARRATOR: Tok felimin dapic-on. Larn mokuretaxu Saukentax hi Dandin kérin recz-ol…
> 
> "The moment of inspection begins. Two recruits stand at the front of the Commander and Ensign…"

> CHEYRIN: Mokure ARÌK, aur ci kérin-jia hemlé-tot, techi?
> 
> "Recruit Arìk, you approached the front, correct?"

> ARÌK: Te- Techi, Saukentax.
> 
> "Co- Correct, Commander."

> CHEYRIN: Ma… aur ci k'áye yanis'dun jimartol-ot, techi. Aur yi ayuga jimartol, techi?
> 
> "But… your Crew's whispers \[rumors] were heard, correct. Was everything heard by me, correct?"

> ARÌK: Te- Techi, Saukentax.
> 
> "Co- Correct, Commander."

> CHEYRIN: Hi BÉBÍ, aur monsi ci hemlétot?
> 
> "And Bébí, when did you approach?"

> BÉBÍ: Aa, sa… Bajia, Saukentax.
> 
> "Ah, umm… Today \[Just now], Commander."

> CHEYRIN: Dandin, bajia kufi jia winirin promeitochi. Jua, bajia kuji jia larinirin promeit-ochi.
> 
> "Ensign, for today, log her day as First Class. Also, for today, log his day as Second Class."

> DARMI: Techi, Saukentax. Promeitot.
> 
> "Yes, Commander. Recorded."

_I am not satisfied I have fully cracked this passage. However my term amongst the Space Latin speakers has come to an end, and I must leave further work in the hands of my successor or successors. _

## Part IV - Liora's Stress Tests

### Sample 31

#### Original Submission

> **ARÌK: Yi Saukentax tak-ton, ma xi yi hemlé marton.**
> 
> _I Commander away-move, but it me towards walks._
> 
> "_I move away from the Commander, but she [the fear of her] walks towards me._"

**Linguistic Note:** Testing the use of `Tak` and `Hemlé` as directional postpositions for abstract concepts (fear/intent). It highlights Arìk's growing paranoia.

#### Revised Submission

> **ARÌK: _Yi Saukentax takton, ma anlama i Saukentax yi hemlé marton._**
> 
> Arík: I Commander away-move, but fear of the Commander walks towards me.
> 
> "**Arìk:** Though I leave the Commander's presence, fear of Her is only walking closer."

### Sample 32

#### Original Submission

> **BÉBÍ:** Aur ci daiti anpera icha? Ci reczot-'rin.
> 
> _[Q] you Ma'am pleased is-not? You stood-well._
> 
> **"Are you [still] not pleased with the Ma'am? You presented yourself well."**

**Linguistic Note:** Bébí uses the honorific `Daiti` as a noun. She also uses the positive quality enclitic `-'rin` to reassure Arìk of his formal performance despite his nerves.

#### Revised Submission

> **BÉBÍ:** _Ci, Daiti, aur lo anpera icha? Aur yi reczot'rin?_
> 
> Bébí: You, Ma'am, \[Q] pleased aren't? \[Q] (Did) I stand-well?
> 
> "_**Bébi:** Ma'am, you... still aren't pleased? Did I stand well?_

### **Sample 33**

#### Original Submission

> **DARMI:** Áye yanis'dun jimartol, ma xi atakama ic.
> 
> _Crew whisper-PL hear-HAB, but it nothing is._
> 
> **"The whispers of the crew are heard, but they are nothing [void/untrue]."**

- **Linguistic Note:** Testing the use of the augmentative prefix `A-` for "Crew" and the habitual/passive `-tol` suffix. Darmi is dismissing the rumors about Cheyrin as empty noise.

#### Revised Submission

> **DARMI:** _Áye yanis'dun jimartol, ma atakama ic._
> 
> Darmi: UNIV.crew rumor hear.PRESPASS, therefore UNIVE-nothing COP
> 
> Darmi: The crew is always hearing rumours, therefore (they) are nothing.

### **Sample 34**

#### Original Submission

> **NARRATOR:** Romurén nikar, jia winirin jua jia larinirin ijintol.
> 
> _Shuttle inside, day first also day second know-PASS._
> 
> **"Inside the shuttle, the first day and the second day are known."**

- **Linguistic Note:** Using `Ijin` in its passive/habitual sense to mean "to be recognized." This reinforces the "marking" Cheyrin performed in the logs—their status as newcomers is now a matter of public record.

#### Revised Submission

> **NARRATOR:** _Romurén nikar, jia winirin hi jia larinirin ijintol._
> 
> Narrator: shuttle inside, day one-ORD and day two-ORD know.PRESPASS
> 
> Narrator: Within the shuttle, the First-Day and the Second-Day are known.

### **Sample 35**

#### Original Submission

> **CHEYRIN:** Dandin, ba mokuretaxu dama lamatot'dul. Shi kucilan folryakun.
> 
> _Ensign, these recruits new scared-much. They themselves stop-FUT/IMP._
> 
> **"Ensign, these new recruits were very scared. They must compose themselves [stop themselves]."**

- **Linguistic Note:** Applying the Class II Future/Imperative (`-yakun`) for a command of self-regulation. It also uses the reflexive `kucilan` to indicate that the recruits must control their own emotions.

#### Revised Submission

> **CHEYRIN:** _Dandin, banu mokuretaxu dama lamatot'dul. Ka kushilan folryakun..._
> 
> Cheyrin: Ensign, this.PL recruit.PL new scare.PAST.AUG if GEN.3N.REFL stop.NEGFUT
> 
> Cheyrin: Ensign, these new recruits were scared intensely. If they do not restrain themselves...

### **Sample Text #36: The Conflict of States**

#### Original Submission

> **ARÌK:** _Yi kinar ic, ma aba aurok midox jun ic._

- **Goal:** Analyze the distinction between the personal state of remorse (_kinar_) and the objective, spatial location of an error (_midox jun_).

#### Revised Submission

> **ARÌK:** _Yi kinar ic, ma aba midox jun ic._
> 
> Arìk: 1SG pathetic COP so UNIV.this error present COP.
> 
> Arìk: I'm worthless, so (I) am the error of all that happened.

#### **Sample Text #37: The Vector of Accountability**

- **Goal:** Test the dual-use of _hemlé_ as both a postpositional vector (modifying _marton_) and a formal Class I verb root (_hemléton_).

#### Revised Submission

> **CHEYRIN:** _Xau Saukentax hemlétolot, ma áye hemlé dekchinu hemlétol._
