### **Further Investigating Sample #1**

Current Unknown: The suffix -tol.

Context: We know _Trininu shoxaton_ means "_Trininu_ sees" (or some similar simple present action). We know _Trininu shoxatol_ is not past tense.

#### Revised Hypothesis for `-tol`

If the distinction is not Past Tense, my next strongest guess for a primary inflectional contrast on a verb in a pair like this is Future Tense.

**Hypothetical Gloss:**

> Trininu shoxa-tol
> 
> Trininu see-FUT
> 
> "Trininu will see."

**Alternative consideration:** If not tense, it could be **Negative** ("Trininu does not see"), but usually negation requires more marked morphology (like a particle or a distinct prefix) than a simple single-letter coda shift. A future/non-past distinction feels more likely for this minimal change.
