# Field Notebook of Dr. Elara Vance

## First Contact

I have found myself among a spacefaring culture of which I have never before heard or known. They appear very human-like, and their manner of speech is phonologically comprehensible to me, however I have no idea what any of it means. Some among them appear able to speak with me, however even these claim that this is through a form of translation matrix rather than truly knowing the English language which I speak.

I am a student in Linguistics from the University of Wisconsin, and though I am still a student, I am a graduate student and believe myself prepared for this first contact field work. Note that as they appear to understand me, but I do not understand most of them, it appears that whatever "translation matrix" technology they use is designed only to translate into their own tongue. My limited knowledge to date has resulted in me proposing the placeholder name, an exonym to be sure, of "Space Latin." This is not because the tongue appears especially to resemble Latin, but because it appears to be a lingua franca across seemingly multiple smaller cultures among the people, with the description of "Space" because I appear to be aboard a spacefaring vessel of some sort.

This is my field notebook. I am assisted by a linguistically informed individual among their people by the name of (and I apologize for my spelling of this): Sorock Grace Lu'amni. I have been told I may call her Grace or Sorock, and I am assured she will not deceive me in my studies, but that she is learning English much as I am learning this language, only she appears more advanced. This makes our efforts a collaboration of sorts, I suppose. I am prepared, at least as much as I believe anybody can be:

I will approach this "Space Latin" as a complete linguistic isolate, making no assumptions about its phonology, syntax, or vocabulary based on Earth languages.

My methodology will be:

1. **Observation:** I will analyze the raw data you provide.
2. **Hypothesis:** I will state what I _think_ is happening (identifying potential roots, affixes, word order, etc.). 
3. **Verification:** I will ask you to confirm or deny these hypotheses.
4. **Codification:** Only confirmed rules will be added to our drafting grammar.
