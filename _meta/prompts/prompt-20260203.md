#### 3. _Exinanitio-Pulchra_ "Beautiful Emptying"

The process of purging the "Old Ego" to replace it with the Will of a Dominant or Master (_soma_). This one is an easy solution:

_Enrokacip Keinau_, "mind-emptying joyful"

This is the process and feeling of divorcing oneself from one's _enros_, one's "own" mind, brain, ego, and sense of self, to become empty. Because the verbal element, _enroskacip_ is "mind-" or "self-" emptying, it is always approaching zero, always active, and always inherently done by and to the same entity. To leave the reflexive state, it must be combined in a construction such as _kempos enrokacip_ "does a brain-draining."

**Somaiti kuyi'hemlé kempos enrokacip keinau.**
"_Mistress does a beautiful ego-draining on me._"

**Yi enrokaciton keinau.**
"_I joyfully empty (my) ego._"
