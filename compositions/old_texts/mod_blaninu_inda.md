# Original Poem: _Blaninu Inda_

This presents a modern translation of [_Inda sug Blaninu_](compositions/old_texts/orig_blani_blani.md), a poem originally composed by the end of 2002.

## Adinjo Text

Ah!

Blani, blani, blani pazo,
Ka ci mordula lixatoç,
Kyi morcin amentoloçi,
Porén qori kuhotan cumastel

Blani, blani, blani vic'i,
Ci kalatax dul'zanbo ic,
Kuci folru hotan'lin ic,
Monsi luxan kujia djoi kalaton.

Blani pazo, blani vic'i:
Ci gurion telu kami!
Ace keinu, telar deirin,
Toshi'lin vic'i, dul'limpa pazo:

Kyi tamanonu ci tonstoç?

## Translated English Text

Oh!

Housecat, housecat, dusky housecat,
If you the slightest sound should make,
My little brother will be awakened,
Having the power of so many tigers.

Housecat, housecat, faded housecat,
You are the most intense hunter,
Your motion is like the tiger,
When he hunts for four days' worth of meals.

Dusky housecat, faded housecat:
Among all beasts the emperor!
Beautiful monster, powerful beast,
One faded and snowy, one dark as a pit:

Will you become my friend?

This poem is an original work written by Austin Springer in 2002. It has been updated on 17-July-2025 to v4 standards, and re-translated back to English in a manner that better preserves the meter of the (new) original.
