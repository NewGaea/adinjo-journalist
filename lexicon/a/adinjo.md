## Noun

### adinjo (plural _adinjonu_)

1. Adin language
	1. any language native to the Adin peoples or derived from such a language
2. _Adinjo Xoltwatax_, Adinjo Journalist
	1. the common language of the Adin Emissaries as preserved for communication across great distances in time and space
