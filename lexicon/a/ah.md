# _ah_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /a.ˈhi/

## Ton Verb

### ah (present _ahton_, past _ahtot_, future _ahtochi_)

1. to journey, to travel, to wander
2. to live by traveling, to live in an itinerant or nomadic manner

### ahi

1. journeying, traveling, wandering
2. living by traveling, living as an itinerant or nomad
