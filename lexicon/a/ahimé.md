# _ahimé_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

Derived from [_ah_](lexicon/a/ah.md) "to journey, to travel, to wander."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /a.hi.ˈme:/

## Noun

### ahimé (plural _ahiménu_)

1. (itinerant) martial artist, (itinerant) monk
	1. one who dedicates their life to strict discipline in the pursuit of spiritual or physical development, often with a degree of deprivation to support such
2. missionary
	1. a religious or philosophical messenger seeking to spread their beliefs or ideals
