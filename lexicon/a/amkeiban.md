## Noun

### amkeiban (plural _amkeibanu_)

1. sliced bread
	1. a loaf of bread which has been cut into uniform pieces by the baker prior to selling it
	2. a loaf of bread which is cut by the baker as a usually free-of-charge service during sale
	3. mass produced bread sold in pre-sliced loaves, often of a softer texture than fresh bread and often also containing preservatives to delay the growth of mold and slow the process of going stale
2. piece of bread, slice of bread
	1. a portion of a loaf that has been separated fully from the loaf, whether by hand or tools
3. sandwich
	1. one slice of bread covered by other food
	2. two or more slices of bread or a split roll having other foodstuffs as filling in the middle
