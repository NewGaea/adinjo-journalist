# _amuran_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

Derived from [_amurent_](lexicon/a/amur.md) "scattering, sowing."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /a.ˈmu.ɾan/

## Noun

### amuran (plural _amuranu_)

1. spring, springtime
	1. a time or season of growth and development
	2. *specifically* the season between [_shuka_](lexicon/s/shuka.md) "winter" and [_girina_](lexicon/g/girina.md) "summer" on Gaea, typically the period of time between the [_Kuha'iluan_](lexicon/k/kuha'iluan) "March" equinox and the [_Xá'iluan_](lexicon/x/xá'iluan.md) "June" solstice in the northern hemisphere
2. planting season, warming season
	1. a time or season when the land is soft enough to be plowed and planted with anticipation of growth
	2. a time or season of comfortable and gradually rising temperatures, usually with a pleasant climate and characterized by mild weather
