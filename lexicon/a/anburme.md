# _anburme_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /an.ˈbuɹ.me/

## Descriptor (Participle)

### anburme

1. baked
	1. having been cooked in an oven
	2. having been dried or hardened by the application of heat
2. cooked
	1. having been prepared for eating by any heating process
	2. having been produced by a planning process
3. roasted
	1. having been cooked in dry heat (as in an oven or by a fire), or by surrounding with heated embers, earth, or stones
	2. having been cooked in dry heat, *especially* with steam from broth and the drippings of a fatty food such as meat
	3. having darkened skin as a result of prolonged exposure to the sun
	4. being developed by one's own difficult experiences
