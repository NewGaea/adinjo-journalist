## Noun

### anjalu'ai (plural anjalu'ainu)

1. mess
	1. a dirty, disordered, untidy, offensive, or unpleasant state or condition
2. dirtiness
	1. a state of being covered in dirt, dust, grime, muck, slime, or other such unsightly or unpleasant materials
