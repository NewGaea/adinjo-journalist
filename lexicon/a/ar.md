## Ton Verb

### ar (present _arton_, past _artot_, future _artoçi_)

1. to fling, to hurl, to throw
2. to launch

### ari

1. flinging, hurling, throwing
2. launching

## Participle

### ar

1. flung, hurled, thrown
2. launched

### anar (gerund _anari_)

1. (be) flung, (be) hurled, (be) thrown
2. (be) launched
