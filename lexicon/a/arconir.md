## 1. Noun

### _arconir_ (plural _arconiru_)

1. bear
	1. an animal of the Ursidae clade, large, heavy, carnivoran mammals with long hair, rudimentary tails, and plantigrade feet which feed omnivorously, primarily on fruit, plant matter, insects, and flesh
	2. one of the twelve ancient tribes of the Adin which lived in the forests of their traditional homeland
		1. a member or descendant of same tribe
	3. a hirsute person, especially one with heavy hair on the arms, chest, back, and belly
2. boast, brag, claim (cn. 2025-Sep-16)
	1. a statement or claim of promise which exceeds one's current ability to fulfill or guarantee
