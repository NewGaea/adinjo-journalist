## Noun

### arlic (plural arlicu)

1. pen
	1. a tool intended for creating (usually indelible) marks with ink or similar fluid (cf. _djeci_ "feather, quill")
	2. a tool for writing in general
	3. the stick or rod used to hold a nib or fine brush for writing
2. stylus, wand
	1. a tool used to press marks into clay or mud, usually with a shaped tip
	2. a pen-shaped stick with a sharp point, used to inscribe wax tablets
	3. a pen-shaped stick with a blunt or rounded point used to signal a touch-sensistive device
3. baton, wand
	1. a tool used to guide the attention of others by being moved about through the air
	2. a tool used to draw attention as a form of performance, often combining elements of dance and acrobatics
4. \[magic] training rod, training staff, training wand
	1. a stick used in the training of magic-users which is constructed in common shapes and sizes, but has no specific magical affinity
