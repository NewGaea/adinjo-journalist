# _arlidjeci_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

From [_arlic_](lexicon/a/arlic.md) "pen" and [_djeci_](lexicon/d/djeci) "feather"

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /aɹ.ˈli.d͡ʒɛ.ˌt͜si/

## Noun

### arlidjeci (plural arlidjecinu)

1. feather pen, quill, quill pen
	1. a pen made with the quill or body of a feather, or to resemble the same
