## Ton Verb

### arus (present _aruston_, past _arustot_, future _arustoçi_)

1. to sit, to squat
	1. to rest on the buttocks or haunches
	2. to assume or maintain a position supported by the feet with knees bent so the buttocks rest on or near the heels
	3. to occupy a space
	4. to rest atop eggs for the sake of incubation or hatching
	5. to affect one with, or as if with, weight
	6. \[fashion] to hang, lie, or rest on a wearer
2. to lounge, to recline
	1. to rest in a position that is neither upright nor prone
	2. to lean or incline backwards, especially in a seat
3. to attend, to meet as a guest, to visit
	1. to attend a session being held

### aruse

1. sitting, squatting
2. lounging, reclining
3. attending, meeting as a guest, visiting
