## Noun

### arush (plural _arushu_)

1. chair, seat, sofa
2. cushion, pillow
3. speech, presentation

## Ton Verb

### arush (present _arushton_, past _arushtot_, future _arushtoçi_)

1. to seat
	1. to offer space for sitting
	2. to provide furnishings for others to sit on
2. to entertain guests, to host, to meet as a host
	1. to provide a venue for people to gather in, whether open to the public or open only by private invitation
	2. to welcome visitors or guests into a place, particularly a place of business or one's home
	3. to take meetings as a host, leader, or authority
3. to present, to speak publicly
	1. to speak or present to an audience as an authority, expert, or otherwise, under the invitation of another

### arushèn

1. seating people
2. entertaining guests, hosting a gathering, meeting as a host
3. presenting, speaking
