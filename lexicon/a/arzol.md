## Descriptor

### arzol

1. blue, cyan, indigo (cb. 2002-Jul-31)
	1. of a color similar in hue to that of the clear Gaean sky or lapis lazuli
	2. a color whose hue falls within the color spectrum between _veron_ and _véol_
		1. in illusions and light-based additive color, a color which is produced with more high-frequency (blue) visible light than short- (red) or mid-frequency (green) visible lights.
2. cheerful, friendly, glad, happy, high-spirited (cn. 2025-Sep-16)
	1. expressing, reflecting, or suggestive of well-being, contentment, or joy
	2. enjoying or characteristic of well-being, contentment, or joy
	3. having or marked by an atmosphere for good fellowship and goodwill
3. noble, royal (cn. 2025-Sep-16)
	1. of quality or characteristics perceived as suitable for the upper or ruling classes of society
4. expensive, rare, valuable (cn. 2025-Sep-16)
	1. of high cost to produce or purchase
	2. infrequent, uncommon
