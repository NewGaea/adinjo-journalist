## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /a.ˈʃaun/

## Noun

### ashaun (plural _ashaunu_)

1. horse
	1. a large solid-hoofed herbivorous ungulate mammal (_Equus caballus_, clade Equidae) which has been domesticated since prehistoric times and used as a beast of burden, a draft animal, and a riding animal.
