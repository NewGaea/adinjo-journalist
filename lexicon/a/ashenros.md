## Noun

### ashenros (plural _ashenrosu_)

1. meditation
	1. the act or process of meditating
	2. a state of heightened mental focus intended to allow deeper thought and self-reflection by the subject
	3. a shared communication produced to express the results of the author's, or orator's, reflections, or to help guide others in reflective contemplation
2. trance
	1. a sleeplike state often characterized by limited animation and diminished or absent sensory activity
	2. a state of profound abstraction, reflection, absorption, or receptiveness
	3. the state of being possessed by a spirit voluntarily
3. hypnosis
	1. a trance or trancelike state that resembles sleep but is induced by a person whose guidance or suggestions are readily accepted by the subject
	2. a trance or trancelike state that resembles sleep
