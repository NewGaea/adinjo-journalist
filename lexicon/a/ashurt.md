## Ton Verb

### ashurt (present _ashurdon_, past _ashurdot_, future _ashurdoçi_)

1. to assault, to attack, to invade, to raid
	1. to attack suddenly with a small force
	2. to attack with the purpose of banditry or theft

### ashurtas

1. assaulting, attacking, invading, raiding
