# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /a.ˈʃuɹ.taþ/

## Noun

### ashurtath (plural ashurtathu)

1. assault, attack, incursion, invasion, raid
	1. a hostile or predatory incursion
	2. a surprise attack by a small force
	3. an attack with the purpose of banditry or theft
