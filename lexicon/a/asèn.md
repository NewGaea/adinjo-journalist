## Ton Verb

### asèn (present _asènton_, past _asèntot_, future _asèntoçi_)

1. to bite
	1. to seize with the teeth or jaws so as to enter, grip, or wound
	2. to wound, pierce, or sting, especially with teeth, fangs, or a proboscis
	3. to tear into as with a serrated weapon
	4. to cause crushing or crippling pain to
	5. to take hold of with a tool that closes like jaws
	6. to accept or respond positively to an offer

### asènru

1. biting
