# _asènger_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

Noun form of [_asèn.ru_](lexicon/a/asèn.md). It is uncertain where the _-ger_ suffix originated.

Possibly related to [_asènt_](lexicon/a/asènt.md) "tenseness of a sound," perhaps via a recent shared root where bite and tenseness were more explicitly connected?

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /a.sɛŋ.ˈger/ ![asenger](audio/01-adob/ogg/asenger.ogg)

## Noun

### asènger (plural _asèngeru_)

1. bite
	1. the act of biting
	2. the manner of biting or shape of one's jaws
	3. an amount of food taken into the mouth
	4. a sharp penetrating quality or effect
	5. a short quotation
	6. expression of interest in an offer
2. bite mark, hickey
	1. a wound made by biting
	2. a temporary red mark or bruise of the skin made by biting, sucking, or a rough grip (especially if developing from intimate conduct)
3. dog-ear
	1. any mark placed on the corner of a page to help a reader identify the last place they stopped reading
	2. a bent, curled, torn, or worn corner of a page, regardless of how the corner received this condition
