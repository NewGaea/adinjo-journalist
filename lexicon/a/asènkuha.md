# _asènkuha_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

Compound of [_asènt_](lexicon/a/asènt) "east" and [_kuha_](lexicon/k/kuha.md) "sun."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /a.sɛn.ˈku.ha/

## Noun

### asènkuha (plural asènkuhanu)

1. dawn, daybreak, sunrise
	1. the period of time when sunlight becomes visible until the sun is risen visibly beyond the horizon, generally until last pink or red tones leave the sky (on Gaea)
2. beginning, start
	1. the threshold or initial point of an adventure, journey, quest, or story
