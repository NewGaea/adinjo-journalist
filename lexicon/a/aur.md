## Pronunciation

### **IPA Broad**

- **Particle, Infinitive**: /ˈauɹ/ ![aur](audio/lexicon/adob/ogg/aur.ogg)
- **Gerund**: /ˈauɹ.en/

## Particle

### aur

1. ask, eh, huh, verbal question indicator
	1. spoken at the start of a clause or phrase to indicate that the following is a question

## Ton Verb

### aur (present _aurton_, past _aurtot_, future _aurtoçi_)

1. to ask, to inquire, to interrogate, to query, to question

### auren

1. asking, inquiring, interrogating, querying, questioning
