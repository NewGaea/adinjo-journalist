# _aurdai_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Internal Etymology

A compound of [_aur_](lexicon/a/aur.md) "to question" and [_dai_](lexicon/d/dai.md) "body, form."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /auɹ.ˈdai/ ![aurdai](audio/lexicon/adob/ogg/aurdai.ogg)

## Noun

### aurdai

1. interrogative mark, question mark
	1. a written symbol denoting the end of a clause or phrase expressing a question
