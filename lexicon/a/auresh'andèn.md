# _auresh'anden_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

Compound word from [_auresh_](lexicon/a/auresh.md) "shirt, tunic" and [_andèn_](lexicon/a/andèn.md) "holy", literally "holy shirt" or "holy tunic."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ˈau.ɾesh.ˌan.dɛn/

## Noun

### auresh'andèn (plural auresh'andènu)

1. button shirt
	1. a shirt which opens in the front and closes with the use of [_menashu_](lexicon/m/menash.md) (buttons) or a similar fastener, with a tail intended to be tucked into the waistline of [_tumar_](lexicon/t/tumar) (trousers)
2. tunicle, vestment
	1. a form of front-opening shirt or vest typically decorated simply and with a stiff collar, worn most typically by clerics or priests performing their formal duties
