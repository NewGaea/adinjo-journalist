## Noun

### auresh'ektar (plural _auresh'ektaru_)

1. tank top
	1. a shirt with minimal or no sleeves
2. tight shirt, undershirt
	1. a shirt designed to hug the upper body closely, usually with the intent of being worn under another shirt or garment
