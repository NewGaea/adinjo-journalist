## Noun

### auresh'tal (plural _auresh'talu_)

1. camisole, little shirt, small shirt
	1. a short, collarless sleeveless shirt or upper body garment, designed for relaxation and ease of movement; often associated as women's clothing by humans
	2. a short jacket or vest usually worn over informal or intimate garments
2. swim shirt
	1. a snugly fitted collarless shirt, with or without sleeves, designed to be worn in water, may be used for reasons of modesty or shame in place of topless or revealing swimwear
