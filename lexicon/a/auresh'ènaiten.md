# _auresh'ènaiten_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

A compound of [_auresh_](lexicon/a/auresh.md) (shirt, tunic) and [_ènaiten_](lexicon/e/ènaiten.md) (unclear gloss).

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ˈau.ɾeʃ.ɛ.ˌnai.ten/

## Noun

### auresh'ènaiten (plural _auresh'ènaitenu_)

1. heavy tunic
	1. a loose fitting tunic which is designed to conceal the body shape of its wearer, usually made of heavier textiles, and suitable for wearing over other garments or armor
2. jumper (UK), sweatshirt (US)
	1. a loose collarless pullover or jacket usually made of heavy cotton
