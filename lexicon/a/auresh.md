# _auresh_

# English

## Alternative Forms

Some speakers pronounce the word as **arèsh**

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

### **IPA Broad**

- Standard Form: /ˈau.ɾeʃ/ ![auresh](audio/01-adob/ogg/auresh.ogg)
- Alternative Form: /a:.ɾɛʃ/

## Noun

### auresh (plural _aureshu_)

1. tunic
	1. a slip-on garment, with or without sleeves, suspended from and covering the upper body and long enough to cover the groin and buttocks, sometimes as long as the knees
	2. a loosely enclosing or covering membrane or tissue
		1. a material used in such a manner
2. blouse, shirt
	1. a garment for the upper part of the body usually having a collar, sleeves, and a tail or hemline long enough to be tucked into the waist of [_tumar_](lexicon/t/tumar.md) (trousers) or a skirt
