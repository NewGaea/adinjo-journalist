# _aurpöéna_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

Possibly a composition of [_aur_](lexicon/a/aur.md) (question) and _\*pöéna_ meaning "is it _pöéna_?" though the root _\*pöéna_ is currently not attested.

### External Etymology

Originally coined as _aurpwéna_ (attested by 2015-Mar-26) and converted to _aurpoéna_ (by 2023-Jan-10). Potentially (likely?) related to [_erapwén_](lexicon/e/erapwén) "flour, ground grain, meal."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ˈauɹ.ˌpoʷe.na/

## Descriptor

### aurpöéna (form inflected-lexeme)

1. filling, satisfying
	1. producing contentment or pleasure by meeting necessity or desire
	2. resolving a need or desire in whole rather than merely in part
2. \[culinary] meal-ish, meal-y, meal-like
	1. a dish which is suitable for service as a full meal without requiring any appetizer, side, or dessert to completely satisfy hunger
	2. a pre-packed dish -- possibly one including appetizer, side, or dessert -- intended to serve as a meal for one person
