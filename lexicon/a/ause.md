## Etymology

### External Etymology

By memory, this word can be traced back to spring or summer of 2005, as it was coined while playing _Nintendogs_ and training a virtual pet using Adinjo. Until other written attestation is found, the earliest clear attestation in writing is apparently from 2008.

## Ton Verb

### ause (present _auseton_, past _ausetot_, future _ausetoçi_)

1. (intransitive) to lie down, to lie flat
	1. to be or stay at rest in a horizontal position, to be prostrate
	2. to assume a horizontal position
	3. to remain inactive, _especially_ while in concealment
	4. \[euphemism] to participate in sexual intercourse, _especially_ followed by sleeping in the same bedding
2. (transitive) to lay down, to place, to put, to set down
	1. to establish a position of an object, particularly with a horizontal orientation
	2. to place for rest or sleep
	3. to release an object from one's grasp onto a surface
	4. to cause an object to occupy a position
		1. to order or organize objects on a surface
	5. to disperse, dispose, or spread over on on a surface
	6. to establish a burden, duty, law, rule, or punishment
	7. to prepare or position something for action, operation, or for later collection
	8. to assert, claim, or submit an object for assessment

### ausene

1. (intransitive) lying down, lying flat
2. (transitive) laying down, placing, putting, setting down
