# _axen_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

Compound of [a-](lexicon/a/a-.md) "absolute" and [_xen_](lexicon/x/xen.md) "end, final, last."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /a.ˈxen/

## Descriptor

### axen

1. final, last, terminal, ultimate
	1. coming at the end; being the last in a series, process, or progression
	2. of or relating to a conclusion from some form of review or deliberation
	3. not to be altered, undone, or continued

Using the intensifier *a-*, there is a stronger sense of finality in the use of _axen_ than there is in the normal use of _xen_. This can be translated into English in some cases by using terms like "absolute" or "very" to modify the English glosses.
