# _axáron_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

Compound of [_a-_](lexicon/a/a-.md) "great, grand" and [_xáron_](lexicon/x/xáron.md) "mage, magic user." Originally coind as _alxáron_ (though formerly written as _alkharon_ or _aløaron_ depending on romanization practices of the time)

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /a.ˈxæː.ɾon/

## Noun

### axáron (plural _axáronu_)

1. archmage, archwizard
	1. a magic user who has attained mastery in four or more of the magical disciplines, such as mastering four elemental styles of magic, four wizardly schools of magic, artifice, alchemy, thaumics, etc. 
2. polymath, sage, savant, virtuoso
	1. an individual with deep knowledge on many different fields of study, comparable to an archmage though these fields need not include anything magical
