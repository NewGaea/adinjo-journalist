# _ayas_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

### IPA Broad
- _infinitive_ /a.jas/
- _gerund_ /a.ja.su.ɾi/

## Ton Verb

### ayas (present _ayaston_, past _ayastot_, future _ayastoçi)

1. to comfort
	1. to ease grief, pain, stress, suffering, or troubles of
	2. to support through difficult emotions
2. to soothe
	1. to please or satisfy with attention or concern
	2. to bring comfort, solace, or reassurance to
3. to calm
	1. to settle the emotions or mental state of
	2. to bring to rest or stillness

### ayasuri

1. comforting
2. soothing
3. calming
