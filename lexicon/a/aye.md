## Noun

### aye (plural _ayénu_)

1. hand
	1. the terminal part of the forearm in vertebrates particularly when modified as a grasping organ; the body part at the end of the arm of primates classified as monkeys and humans, as well as similarly shaped sapient species
	2. assistance or aid of a physical or emotional nature
	3. participation or engagement with
	4. skill or ability, especially at physical tasks
	5. an instance of handiwork, such as:
		1. the style of production or execution of a task
		2. the results of some labor or task
		3. the manner or style of writing or illustration
2. holdings, inventory, stock
	1. property or items possessed on one's person or near to hand
	2. property or items held in storage but accessible for use
	3. property or items possessed legally but not readily accessible at a given time
	4. a collection of game pieces currently possessed by a given person
3. crew member, employee, laborer, worker
	1. a person employed in physical labor or general tasks
	2. a member of a group engaged in collective effort, including:
		1. a member of a [_dandè_](lexicon/d/dandè.md), guild, society, or union
		2. a member of an adventuring party or military unit
	3. a person who performs or executes a specific instance of work or production
