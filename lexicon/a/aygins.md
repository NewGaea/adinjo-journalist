## Pronunciation

### **IPA Broad**

- _infinitive_ /aʲ.ˈgins/
- _gerund_ /aʲ.ˈgin.sten/

## Ton Verb

### aygins (present _ayginston_, past _ayginstot_, future _ayginstoçi_)

1. to play
	1. to take part in a recreational activity or frivolity
	2. to toy or fiddle about with something
	3. to behave in a light, speculative, or teasing manner
	4. to exploit the similarities of two (or more) things for stylistic or humorous effect
2. to act, to perform
	1. to perform a role on stage, or as if on stage
	2. to perform publicly for the sake of entertaining others
	3. to behave in a particular manner, _especially_ one inconsistent with one's normal character
3. to do a hobby, to perform a pursuit of passion
	1. to explore an interest for recreation or personal enjoyment, especially when heedless of other motivations
	2. to do something, often privately, for comfort, joy, or pleasure

### ayginsten

1. to play (a game)
2. to act or perform (on stage)
3. to do a hobby, to perform a pursuit of passion
