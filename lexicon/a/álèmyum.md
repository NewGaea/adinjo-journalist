# _álèmyum_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

Borrowed from Latin _allium_ "garlic."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ˈæ.lɛ.mʲum/

## Noun

### álèmyum (plural _álèmyumu_)

1. allium
	1. any member of a large genus of bulbous flowering herbs of the amaryllis family
2. chive
	1. a perennial plant of genus Allium (_Allium schoenoprasum_) having slender leaves which are often used as an herbal seasoning
3. garlic
	1. a European plant of genus Allium (_Allium sativum_) widely cultivated for its pungent compound bulbs, used widely as a seasoning in cooking
	2. a bulb of the same plant
4. green onion
	1. a young onion (cf. sense 6) pulled before the bulb has enlarged, used especially in fresh dishes such as salads
5. leek
	1. a biennial herbaceous plant of genus Allium (_Allium porrum_) of the amaryllis family, commonly grown for its mildly pungent leaves and especially for its cylindrical, stemlike lower sheath of leaves
6. onion
	1. a widely cultivated Asian herbaceous plant of genus Allium (_Allium cepa_) of the amaryllis family which has a pungent edible bulb
	2. _also_ the bulb of the onion plant
	3. any allium closely related to the onion
7. scallion
	1. an onion forming a thick basal portion without a bulb
8. shallot
	1. a perennial onion (cf. sense 6; _Allium cepa var. aggregatum_) that produces small clustered bulbs which resemble those of garlic and are used in cooking
	2. _also_ a bulb of the same plant
