# _ba_

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ba/

## Descriptor

### ba (plural _banu_)

1. this
	1. being a person, thing, or idea that is most near in place, time, or thought within discourse
	2. the most recent topic of the present discourse
	3. introducing a new person, thing, or idea that is immediately relevant to discourse, especially in narrative
	4. the most proximate example of something being observed or discussed
	5. the degree, extent, or quality indicated by a following descriptor within discourse and context

## Pronoun

### ba (plural _banu_)

1. this
	1. a type of person or a thing or idea that is most near in time or place within discourse
		1. (_before a verb of communication_) an introduction of a quotation or quotable statement
	2. the present time or place
	3. a referent nearer in place to the speaker than to another interlocutor
	4. the thing or idea most recently referenced
