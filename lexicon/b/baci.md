# _baci_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ba.ˈt͜si/

## Ton Verb

### baci (present _baciton_, past _bacitot_, future _bacitochi_)

1. to see
	1. to perceive by eye
	2. to perceive or detect _as if_ by sight
		1. to be aware of
		2. to imagine as possible
		3. to form a mental image
		4. to perceive meaning or importance of
	3. to witness as an observer
	4. to regard as
	5. to call upon or visit
		1. to keep company with, *especially* in courtship or dating
		2. to grant audience with
	6. to look about
		1. to pay attention
	7. to acknowledge or consider something
2. to observe
	1. to look at or watch carefully, especially with attention to details
	2. to form a conclusion from consideration of facts
	3. to note a fact whether mentally or verbally
3. to view
	1. to look at attentively
	2. to appreciate the visible qualities of
	3. to perceive or interpret in a given light
	4. to examine or survey mentally or visually
4. to watch
	1. to witness as a spectator
	2. to keep sight upon
	3. to monitor for visual changes
	4. to be on alert for

### bacire

1. seeing, viewing
2. observing, watching
