# _baik_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

Borrowed from Modern English _bike,_ by members of the Adin Diplomatic Mission.

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /baik/

## Noun

### baik (plural baiku)

1. bicycle, bike, moped, motorcycle, motor scooter, tricycle
	1. a vehicle with two wheels in tandem and propelled by pedals, usually with handlebars for steering and a saddle seat
		1. a stationary machine for exercise which resembles such a vehicle
		2. a similar vehicle though with 3 wheels (1 in the front and 2 in the rear) for stability, often used by those with stability concerns
		3. a vehicle of similar shape to the above, but motorized
2. pedal-vehicle
	1. any type of vehicle powered by the use of pedals
