# _bajia_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

Compound of [_ba_](lexicon/b/ba.md) "this" with [_jia_](lexicon/j/jia.md) "day."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ba.ˈʒiʲa/

## Noun

### bajia (inherent singular)

1. today
	1. the present or current day
2. now
	1. the present moment
	2. the present age or era
