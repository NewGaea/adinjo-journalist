## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /bak/

## Noun

### bak (plural _baku_)

1. card
	1. a flat, small, sturdy material (such as paper, parchment, cardboard, or plastic) with writing or other markings on it bearing information or imagery
	2. a token depicting a value or character which may be used in divination or games
2. picture
	1. a fixed visual depiction or representation of some person, place, item, or idea
		1. _especially_ such created at a portable size
	2. a transitory visual image or reproduction
	3. a striking or artistic grouping or staging
3. sprite
	1. a mobile image within a programmed illusion or other software
