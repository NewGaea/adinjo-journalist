# _bakato_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

Unknown. Folk etymology claims the word to be something like "cook and serve" though it shows no similarity to [_burmelas_](lexicon/b/burme.md) "to bake, to cook," nor is there a currently attested work for "serving food." It appears to be a truncated conjugation of a ton verb.

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ba.ka.ˈto/

## Expression

### bakato

1. bite me, buzz off, fuck off, sod off
	1. an expression of contempt and dismissal, generally informal and potentially considered rude
