# _bakira_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ba.ˈki.ɾa/

## Conjunction

### bakira

1. also, by the way, furthermore, therefore

## Expression

### bakira

1. as seen, clearly, obviously, understandably

This expression is often used in speech to attempt to assert or reclaim attention of interlocutors as one prepares to address conclusions or make important points. This may also be related in some manner to [_bacire_](lexicon/b/baci.md) "to see, to observe, to view, to watch."
