## Noun

### bal (plural _balu_)

1. beat, rhythm, tempo
	1. a sound or other vibration produced by or as if by beating or heavy impacts
	2. a pulsing sound or vibration
	3. a driving impact or force
	4. a metrical or rhythmic stress pattern in linguistics
		1. _especially_ one in poetry or music
		2. the tempo as which a musical performer plays their part
		3. the overall rhythm serving as a driving force (sense 1c) within certain types of music
		4. the musical backing element of an amelodic rhyme performance such as rap
		5. a sense of or for such stress patterns
	5. \[dance] a heavy, often audible, landing of one foot or hand against any surface during a dance performance (cf. "clap")
	6. a short pause longer than a breath and often indicated by visual or other contextual cues to be deliberate
2. swing
	1. a pulsing rhythm, as in poetry or music, that is consistently irregular but defines the motion of a composition or performance
	2. a sweeping back and forth motion of the body, especially in dance
	3. the state of immersion in a musical performance, _especially_ when one is moved by said music to dancing
		1. a dance performed in such a state
3. pace, step, stride
	1. the length of space between two footfalls of the same foot
	2. the speed at which footfalls occur while moving across solid surfaces
