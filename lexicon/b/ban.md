# _ban_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ban/

## Noun

### ban (plural _banu_)

1. bread
	1. a usually baked food made of a mixture of leavened flour or meal
	2. those things necessary for maintaining life
	   > _**ban** jiabi_ "daily bread"
    > _**ban** hi aurok_ "bread and a room"
2. a meal
	1. the act, or time spent for such act, of eating a portion of food to satisfy an appetite or hunger
	2. a portion of food eaten at or as a meal (sense 2a)
3. \[cuisine] (optionally as a suffix) filled bread, pie, sandwich, wrap
	1. a dish in which bread is a primary component
	   > _**ban** conyìhon hi fidhan_ "ham and cheese sandwich"
	   > _fáshim**ban**_ "apple pie"
