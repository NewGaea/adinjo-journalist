# _banbor_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

A compound of [_banc_](lexicon/b/banc) "to give" and [_bor_](lexicon/b/bor) "to receive."

## Pronunciation

### **IPA Broad**
- _infinitive_ /ban.ˈboɹ/
- _gerund_ /ban.ˈbo.ɾak/

## Ton Verb

### banbor (present _banborton_, past _banbortot, future  _banbortochi_)

1. to barter, to exchange, to swap, to trade
	1. to offer some item, good, skill, or service for another
	2. to replace one thing with another, *especially* of similar value or properties
2. (with [_hemlé_](lexicon/h/hrmlé.md)) to buy, to purchase, to shop
	1. to give money in exchange items, goods, skills, or services
3. (with [_tak_](lexicon/t/tak.md)) to sell
	1. to offer some sort of items, goods, skills, or services in exchange for money
4. (often with [_hort_](lexicon/h/hort.md) or [_takem_](lexicon/t/takem.md)) to have dealings with, to do business with
	1. keeping or upholding a professional relationship based upon exchange of items, goods, skills, or services, whether in kind or for currency
