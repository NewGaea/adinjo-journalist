# _banc_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

Originally coined prior to 2003-Dec-25 as _borchton_, was already changed to _banc.els_ during Version 1 when the name "Nathaniel" (Hebrew for "Gift from God") was translated as _Banchiel_. The current [_borch.ent_](lexicon/b/borch) "to send, to reveal, to inspire" is related, and was in use by 2007-11-26.

## Pronunciation

### IPA Broad

- _infinitive_ /bant͜s/
- _gerund_ /ban.ˈt͜sel/

## Ton Verb

### banc (present _bancon_, past _bancot_, future _bancochi_)

### bancels

1. giving
