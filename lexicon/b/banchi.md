# _banchi_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ban.ˈt͜ʃi/

## Noun

### banchi (plural _banchinu_)

1. gift, present
	1. something voluntarily transferred by one person to another without compensation
	2. something introduced in a hidden state and later revealed to others, often in order to evoke emotions such as surprise and joy
