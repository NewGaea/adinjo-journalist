# _banij_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

Compound of [_ba_](lexicon/b/ba.md) "this" and [_nij_](lexicon/n/nij.md) "place."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ba.ˈnij/

## Descriptor

### banij

1. here
	1. in or at a particular location
	2. at, in, or on a particular detail

## Pronoun

1. here
	1. this location or place
	2. this idea or sentiment
