## Pronunciation

## Noun

### banklaf (plural _banklafu_)

1. celebration, festival
	1. a time of celebration marked by special observances
		1. *especially* one marked by the inclusion of feasting
	2. a periodic course of specially observed events or entertainment with a particular focus
2. holiday, holiday season
	1. a day or days of political, social, or religious significance often marked by exemption from labor or schooling obligations
		1. the period of time surrounding such, as marked by anticipatory or preparatory mood
