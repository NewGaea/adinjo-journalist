# _baru_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ba.ˈɾu/

## Noun

### baru (plural _barunu_)

1. dust
	1. fine particles of solid matter
	2. particles left behind after decomposition or disintegration
2. dirt, infertile soil
	1. loose packed soil or sand having low nutrient quality and moisture
3. powder
	1. matter in a fine particulate state
		1. a preparation of such, *especially* for cosmetic, medicinal, or thaumatic use
		2. a coating of such matter on an object
		3. any of various solid explosives, especially those used for demolition or propulsion of certain ballistic missiles
