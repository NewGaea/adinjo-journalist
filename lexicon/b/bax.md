## Ton Verb

### bax (present _baxton_, past _baxtot_, future _baxtochi_)

1. to grow (upwards), to increase
	1. to increase size in a vertical direction by assimilation or accretion of material
	2. to increase in quantity or amount
	3. to experience an increasing change in state
		1. to have increasing impact or influence
		2. to become increasingly comfortable
2. to cultivate, to farm, to raise
	1. to cause or tend to the growth of
	2. to promote development of
	3. to increase the size of some portion of the body
3. to ascend, to climb, to rise
	1. to assume an upright position, _especially_ from kneeling, lying down, or sitting
	2. to move in a vertical direction
		1. to move up or down along a surface, especially by clutching, grasping, or holding with the hands
		2. to extend above other objects
		3. to increase fervor or intensity, _especially_ of emotional state
		4. to increase in a particular measurement
		5. to increase in level, power, or rank
		6. to exert oneself in the face of challenge or difficulty
	3. to don clothing or armor in haste
	4. to transition from a passive state to a more active state
		1. to be moved to action through pathos
		2. to move to violence

### baxat

1. growing (vertically), increasing
2. cultivating, farming, raising
3. ascending, climbing, rising

## Participle

Note: Because the stem _bax_ has only one vowel, the standard participle matches the standard active conjugations.

## anbax (gerund _anbaxat_)
