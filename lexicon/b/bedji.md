# _bedji_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /be.ˈd͡ʒi/

## Noun

### bedji (plural _bedjinu_)

1. beard
	1. hair which grows on the face and sometimes neck, often excluding hair which grows between the mouth and nose
	2. a hairy, bristly, or keratinous appendage or tuft around the face, _especially_ along the jaw or chin
	3. \[slang] a patch of moss or lichen
2. facial hair
	1. a general term for all hair which grows on the face and sometimes neck, _especially_ when a beard is present
3. front garden, planter box
	1. a collection of plants grown by the front or main entrance or window of a house, especially colorful or aromatic plants
