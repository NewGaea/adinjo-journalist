# English

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /be.ˈɾik/

## Noun

### berik (plural _beriku_)

1. castle
	1. a large fortified building or set of buildings
	2. a massive or imposing house
2. fort, fortress
	1. a strong or fortified location
		1. _especially_ one occupied by armed troops and surrounded with defensive works such as a ditch, rampart, or parapet
	2. a permanent military post
	3. a settlement arising from a location as in 2a or 2b
