### External Etymology

From [Reddit's Biweekly Telephone Game #700](https://www.reddit.com/r/conlangs/comments/1ml0n1s/biweekly_telephone_game_v3_700/). Borrowed from [Knasesj](https://www.reddit.com/r/conlangs/comments/1ml0n1s/comment/n7n51mh/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=web3x&utm_name=web3xcss&utm_term=1&utm_content=share_button): 

> **bevakgyem** [ˈbe.væʔˌɟi͡em] _n._ spring (season)
> 
> Roughly, 'variable skies'. From _bevak_ 'change over time, vary' and _gyem_ 'atmosphere above us, "sky" as a place (but not including things above the atmosphere'.

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /be.'væ:.xʲɛmn/

## Noun

### beváxyemn (plural _beváxyemnu_)

1. squall, monsoon
	1. a strong storm, especially one originating from an ocean or sea
2. flood, inundation
3. tantrum
