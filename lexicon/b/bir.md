# _bir_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

## IPA Broad

- _infinitive_ /biɹ/
- _gerund_ /biɹ.mak/

## Ton Verb

### bir (present _biron_, past _birot_, future _birochi_)

1. to complete, to end, to finish
	1. to bring to an end
	2. to bring a project or issue to a close
		1. to provide with a final detailing pass
		2. to apply a finishing process such as glazing, polishing, or varnishing
	3. to defeat or ruin utterly and finally
		1. to render incapable of resistance
		2. to bring to death
	4. to come to an end
	5. to be at the end
	6. to conclude (often followed by [_hort_](lexicon/h/hort.md) "with")

### birmak

1. completing, ending, finishing
