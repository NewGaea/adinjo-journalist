## bironga

### bironga (plural _bironganu_)

1. finish, finishing touch
	1. something which completes or perfects, such as:
		1. fine decorative work
		2. finishing material such as glaze, polish, or varnish
		3. final treatment or coating of a surface
		4. the taste in the mouth after swallowing food or beverage (aftertaste)
	2. final or terminal stage, _especially_ preceding a failure or ruin
	3. a quality or element resulting from a finishing process
	4. the quality or state of being complete or perfected
2. finale
	1. the close or termination of something, such as:
		1. the closing act, scene, song, or words of some performance, or:
			1. the final chapter or page of a traditional story
			2. the final chapter or episode of a serial story
			3. the ending of a game
			4. the punchline of a joke
		2. the closing of a speech or narrative, _often_ including a summary, moral lesson, or call to action
