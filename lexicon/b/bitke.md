# _bitke_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /bit.ˈke/

## Noun

### bitke (plural _bitkénu_)

1. door
	1. a usually sliding or swinging barrier by which a way of entry may be closed or opened
		1. a similar part of a piece of furniture
	2. a barrier to access or participation
2. gate, heavy gate, portcullis
	1. a movable barrier placed within a wall or fence
	2. the section of a wall with such as sense 2a, often including other defensive structures or works
	3. a secure means of entrance or exit
		1. an area (as at a station or port) designated for cleared individuals to wait for access to a vehicle
		2. a barrier through which passage is permitted in only one direction
	4. \[place name] a settlement or fortification serving as a sole means of access between two areas
	5. a door, valve, or other device to control passage _especially_ of fluids
3. lock
	1. a chamber designed to isolate two environments, _especially_ where one environment has dangerous conditions

	   > [_**bitke**rom_](lexicon/b/bitkerom.md) "airlock"
	   > [_**bitke**chon_](lexicon/b/bitkechon.md) "sealock"

4. safety
	2. an element of a device which serves to regulate some other functionality of the device
