# _bitkechon_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

A compound of [bitke](lexicon/b/bitke.md) "door, lock" and [_chon_](lexicon/c/chon.md) "ocean, sea."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /bit.ˈke.t͜ʃon/

## Noun

### bitkéchon (plural _bitkéchonu_)

1. sealock
	1. a chamber designed to separate water of two different qualities, such as elevation or salinity
