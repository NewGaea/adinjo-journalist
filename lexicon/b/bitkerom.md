# _bitkerom_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

A compound of [_bitke_](lexicon/b/bitke.md) "door, lock" and [_rom_](lexicon/r/rom) "air."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /bit.ˈke.ɾom/

## Noun

### bitkerom (plural _bitkeromu_)

1. airlock
	1. a chamber designed to separate an air-filled environment from another environment, such as between a sterile environment and a contaminated one, or between a breathable space and an environment without breathable air
