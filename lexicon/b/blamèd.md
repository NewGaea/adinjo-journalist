## Noun

### blamèd (plural _blamèdu_)

1. block
	1. a piece of stone, wood, or otherwise a single piece of material shaped, usually by carving, into a fitted shape for construction
	2. a compact, usually solid, piece of substantial material, especially when worked or altered to a particular purpose
	3. a mold, form, or platform on which objects are shaped or displayed
		1. the platform on which an item is presented during an auction
	4. a lightweight small piece of wood used as a building toy
	5. an often anchored point from which racers begin
	6. a space in a town or city enclosed by streets and occupied or intended for occupation by buildings
		1. a measurement based upon the length of such a block
		2. a line of adjacent houses occupying such a block
	7. a large building divided into separate functional units
		1. a distinctive part of a building (cf: wing)
2. brick
	1. a unit of building or paving material which is usually joined together in tight formation or with the use of tar or mortar
		1. such a unit which is usually rectangular and made in large batches of uniform size, often made of moist clay or mud which is baked hard
		2. such a unit which is shaped and fitted to purpose, often at the site of construction
	2. a cubic or rectangular mass of any material packed together, especially in a bound or compressed state
