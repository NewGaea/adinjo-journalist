# _blani'urdin_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

From [_blani_](lexicon/b/blani.md) "cat," [_urda_](lexicon/u/urda) "like, similar to," and [_din_](lexicon/d/din) "person."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /bla.ˈni.ʔur.ˌdin/

## Noun

### blani'urdin (plural _blani'urdin_)

1. cat-person, cat-like person
	1. a person having some features or characteristics like a [_blani_](lexicon/b/blani.md)
2. catboy, catgirl
	1. an illustrated or fictional character depicted as mostly anthropoform, but possessing certain feline traits such as ears, tail, claws, or vertically slitted eyes
	2. a shapeshifter in such a form
3. catfolk, cat furre
	1. a fantasy species of anthropomorphic felines or highly felinized anthropoforms
4. [Andurusian](lexicon/a/anduruisidin.md), [Elressie](lexicon/e/elressidin.md), [Woren](lexicon/w/woredin.md)
	1. any of the known species of anthropomorphic felines or highly felinized anthropoforms
