## Pronunciation

## Noun

### blani (plural _blaninu_)

1. cat, felid
	1. a carnivorous mammal of family _Felidae_, usually solitary and nocturnal predators such as the domestic cat, _jubachi_ (lion), _hotan_ (tiger), leopard, jaguar, cougar, wildcat, lynx, and cheetah.
	2. a creature resembling cats or Felids, even if it not strictly related to them
2. cat, housecat
	1. a small carnivorous mammal long (_Felis catus_) domesticated and kept as a pet or for the catching of rodents
3. hunter, player
	1. an individual engaged in hunting
	2. a person who is looking for romantic or sexual flings, but not long-term commitment
