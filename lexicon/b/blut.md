## Etymology

## Pronunciation

## Positional

### blut

1. down
	1. in, at, or to a lower position
	2. a direction opposite of [_klor_](lexicon/k/klor.md) (up)
	3. to the south or west
	4. lesser or lower in quantity or quality
	5. occurring at or coming from an earlier time
	6. the direction in which gravity attacts most strongly
2. below, beneath, under
	1. in or at a lower position
	2. on a surface as opposed to in the air or heavens
		1. lower than the surface of water
		2. in or to the underworld or afterlife
	3. to a surface at a lower elevation or position, or with a lower assigned level number
	4. in, to, at, by, or for a lower rank or number
	5. having a lesser quantity or quality assigned
	6. in the direction from which a river flows
	7. unworthy of consideration or action
	8. in a submissive or obedient position
