# _bra_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Internal Etymology

Derived from [Proto-Adinjo](_meta/archive/old_documents/fifty_roots_proto.md) _\*bural_ "lead (metal)." Confer with [_bralyumé_](lexicon/b/bralyumé) "leaden, made of lead."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /bɹa/

## Descriptor

### bra

1. heavy, weighted
	1. of a great weight
	2. of an unusual weight
2. burdensome
	1. causing stress or pressure on the heart or mind
3. contemplative, deep, reflective
	1. being in or related to a state of internal focus and thought
