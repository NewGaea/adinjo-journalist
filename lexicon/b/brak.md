# _brak_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**
- _infinitive_ /bɾak/
- _gerund_ /bɾa.ˈkeʃ/

## Ton Verb

### brak (present _brakton_, past _braktot_, future _braktolochi_)

1. lift, pick up
	1. elevate, raise
		1. to move from a lower to a higher position
		2. to increase in assessment, ranking, or rating _especially_ of condition
		3. to increase in rate or amount
	2. to take away
		1. revoke, rescind
			1. to remove a law, restriction, or rule (revoke, rescind)
			2. to remove a burden or obligation
		2. steal
			1. to take unlawfully
		3. plagiarize
			1. to copy or quote without citation
2. accentuate
	1. to draw attention to, _especially_ to promote changes as described in sense 1.a

### brakesh

1. lifting, picking up
2. elevating, raising

## Participle

The simple participle is the same as the standard tenses.

### anbrak (gerund _anbrakesh_)

1. be lifted, be picked up
2. be elevated, be raised
