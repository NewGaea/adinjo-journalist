## Noun

### bralum (plural _bralumu_)

1. lead
	1. a soft, heavy, metallic element (Atomic number ${6X_{12}}$, ${82_{10}}$) found primarily in combination, and used in alloys, as an insulating element for batteries (magical and dry cell), and for shields against radiation, divination magic, mental magic, and psychic abilities; very often used following another noun as a descriptive noun
	   > _kasul bralum_ "lead hat"
	   > _rukam bralum_ "lead shield"
	 2. a metal or metallic weight _especially_ one suspended on a thread or chain, used for both vertical measurements and measurements of verticality
	 3. an object made of lead or of similar qualities, such as:
		 1. a sheet of metal often used for inscription training
		 2. a stick of soft metal or mineral used for impermanent writing
		 3. a type block used in a printing press, _especially_ a blank block used for spacing
		 4. the block tip of a mechanical typewriter which strikes against paper (or other material) to leaves markings
2. metal
	1. any of various opaque, fusible, ductile, and typically lustrous substances that are typically good conductors of heat and usually either magic or electricity
	   _especially_: one that is a chemical element rather than a compound or alloy
	2. glass, _especially_ in a molten state or as stained glass
	3. an inscription etched into metal (sense a)
