# _bralyumé_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

Related to [bralum](lexicon/b/bralum.md) "lead" presumably through some undocumented historical process.

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /bɾa.lʲu.ˈme/

## Descriptor

### bralyumé

1. leaden
	1. made of [lead](lexicon/b/bralum.md)
	2. having qualities like those of [lead](lexicon/b/bralum.md)
2. metal, metallic
	1. made of any metal or alloy
	2. having qualities like those of metals
