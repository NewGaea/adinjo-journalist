## Ton Verb

### brau (present _brauton_, past _brautot_, future _brautochi_)

1. to blow, to breathe out, to exhale
	1. to move air outward through the mouth or nose
	2. to produce sound on a wind instrument
	3. to move something with a current of air
	4. to fill something with air _especially_ when breathed from the lungs
	5. to lose something as if to thin air (usually with _tak_ "away, from")
	6. to focus carefully, _especially_ when under pressure
2. to huff, to pant, to puff
	1. to breathe heavily, _usually_ from exertion or discomfort
	2. to express anger, indignation, or scorn
3. \[euphemism] to burp, to fart
	1. to pass gas through the mouth or anus
	2. (burp) to express satisfaction with food or drink
	3. (fart) to fade from memory, _especially_ in a short time

### braujen

1. blowing, breathing out, exhaling
2. huffing, panting, puffing
3. \[euphemism] burping, farting
