# _braubrú_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

Derived from [_brau_](lexicon/b/brau.md) "burp, fart"

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /f.netiks/

## Noun

### braubrú (plural _braubrúnu_)

1. blast or burst (of breath), gust
	1. a deep and forceful breath out
	2. an exceptionally loud or strong tone from a wind instrument
	3. an abnormal distending of the body
	4. exceptional focus, often to the exclusion of unfocused stimuli
2. huffing and puffing, ranting, venting
	1. the act of letting out frustrations, _especially_ without pause for breath or response
3. \[euphemism] belch, trumpet
	1. a particularly loud or deep passing of gas, whether a burp or fart
