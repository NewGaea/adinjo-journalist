## Pronunciation

### **IPA Broad**
- _infinitive_ /bɹon/
- _gerund_ /bɹon.ˈɾu/

## Ton Verb

### bron (present _bronton_, past _brontot_, future _brontochi_)

1. to delay, to hold (a position)
2. to pause
3. to stay, to stop
4. to wait
 
### bronru

1. delaying, holding (position or location)
2. pausing
3. staying, stopping
4. waiting
