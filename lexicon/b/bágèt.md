## Noun

### bágèt (plural _bágètu_)

1. baguette, bakery-style loaf of bread, italian bread
	1. a long thin loaf of bread or roll, _especially_ one with a soft composition that can be torn apart by hand, but firm enough to handle sandwich making when split or sliced
2. \[sandwich] grinder, hero, hoagie, Italian sandwich, po'boy, sub, submarine, torpedo
	1. a large sandwich on a long split loaf or roll with any variety of fillings, usually including meat, cheese, and vegetable components
