# _bánden_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ˈbæn.den/

## Noun

### bánden (plural _bándenu_)

1. cider, wine
	1. fermented juice of any fruit, sometimes made sparkling by carbonation or fermentation in a sealed container
	2. a fermented alcoholic beverage in general
	3. a beverage which may invigorate or intoxicate
	4. a dark red or orange color, traditionally produced by using old _bándèn_ as a dye
