## Noun

### béra (plural _béranu_)

1. grape
	1. a smooth-skinned juicy berry, usually light green, deep red, or purplish black, edible fresh or dried, and often used as the basic juice source for wines
	2. any of numerous woody vines of genus _Vitis_ that usually climb by tendrils, produce grapes, and can be cultivated in almost all environments
2. berry (especially as a suffix)
	1. a pulpy and usually edible fruit of small size, regardless of its specific shape or structure
	2. a simple fruit with a pulpy or fleshy pericarp
