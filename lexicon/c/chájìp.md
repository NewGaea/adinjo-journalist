## Noun

### chájìp (plural _chájìpu_)

1. (_noun_) ficus, fig
    1. any member of genus Ficus, chiefly tropical trees, shrubs, and woody vines of the mulberry family
        1. _especially_ the tree _Ficus carica_
    2. the fruit of such plants
2. (_noun_) care, concern    
    1. marked attention or interest towards
    2. responsibility for the health, well-being, and safety of
    3. regard coming from desire or esteem
    4. a matter or topic under consideration or discussion
        1. _especially_ such that causes apprehension or unease
