# _colenx_

# English

## Etymology

A compound word formed from _cori_ "some" and _enx_ "head", referring the buds on top of the stalk. 

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /t͜sol.ɛnx/

## Noun

### _colenx_ (pl. _colenxu_)

1. asparagus
