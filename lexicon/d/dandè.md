# _dandè_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /dan.dɛ/

## 1. Noun

### *dandè* (plural *dandènu*)

1. circle, loop, ring
	1. a geometric figure defined by a center point with a single smooth curve of uniform, or assumed uniform, radius
	2. a shape resembling or approximating the same, especially one which is treated as such
2. \[social] circle, coterie, group, guild, order, organization, society, union
	1. a group of individuals who share an interest
	2. a group of workers in the same or similar craft, profession, or trade
	3. a group with shared ideals and interests in knowledge, especially esoteric or mystical knowledge

## 2. Descriptor

### _dandè_

1. consistent
	1. marked by the quality of regular patterns or textures, harmony, or steady continuity
	2. free from variation or contradiction
	3. marked by agreement, usually used with _hort_
	4. showing steady or reliable conformity to character, profession, belief, or custom
	5. tending to be reliably but arbitrarily close to an estimated or calculated parameter
2. smooth
	1. having a continuous even surface
	2. having straight, even hair or fur
		1. [_keng dandè_](lexicon/collocations/keng_dandè.md): clean, short haircut
	3. having no hair or protruding fibers
	4. causing little or no resistance to sliding
	5. free from creases, lumps, or wrinkles
		1. [_dandè kason_](lexicon/collocation/dandè_kason.md) (_kun verb_) to smooth, to make smooth, to make flat
	6. \[social] calm, comfortable, courteous, pleasant
		1. [_dandé yani.s_](lexicon/collocations/dandè_yani.md) (_ton verb_) charming talk, pleasant talk
	7. \[culinary] without bite, spice, tang, or zest
3. \[maths] equal, even, uniform
	1. a group or feature having, or defined by, identical difference between instances or regular repetition thereof
4. \[maths] absolute (value), non-negative (value)
	1. any numerical value of zero or higher, a natural number
	2. the distance from zero to a number, regardless of the direction from zero
