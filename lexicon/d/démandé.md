# _démandé_

# English

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### Internal Etymology

A compound word formed from _dema_ "to command" and _nandé_ "to list."

## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /ˈde.man.ˌde/

## Noun

### démandé (plural _démandénu_)

1. command list, command menu
2. \[computing] menu
