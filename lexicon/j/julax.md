## Ton Verb

### julax (pres. *julaxton*, past *julaxtot*, fut. *julaxtoçi*)
1. to alter, to adjust (transitive)
	1. generally refers to superficial alterations, trimming, clipping, grooming of the hair and body, etc.
2. to change (transitive)
	1. generally refers to removing things in a way that alters the quality of what a thing is; for example, removing the skin of an animal and turning it into leather
3. to transform, to metamorphose
	1. generally intransitive, used to refer to a change of abstract qualities or a restructuring of the body

### julaxan (gerund)
1. altering, adjusting (transitive)
2. changing (transitive)
3. transforming, metamorphosing (intransitive)

## Noun

### julax (pl. *julaxu*)
1. alteration, adjustment
2. change
3. season
	1. a specific period of time characterized by generally similar weather and climate trends
	2. a specific period of time characterized by generally similar mental or emotional experiences
4. stage (of life)
	1. any particular developmental phase in a life as defined by physical or biochemical status
