## Noun

### jènjal (pl. *jènjalu*)
1. hamlet, settlement, village
	1. refers to small settlements, usually with 500 or fewer residents, without organized guard forces, or both. Residents of *jènjalu* usually have large plots of land used for farming of plants or animals, or smaller plots near the crossroads where they ply a trade or craft for the benefit of the community
2. colony
	1. refers specifically to a settlement claimed by an outside nation which has limited fortifications
