# _neim_

# English

## Alternative Forms

- neym (alternate)
- ném (Artexan)

## Etymology

Uncertain. Alleged acronym for _nuqe elaure ijin mikto_ "to seek, to steward, knowledge is might."

Attested by 2008-06-22.
## Pronunciation

- **IPA Broad**: /'neim/

## 1. Noun

### *neim* (pl. *neimu*)

1. knight
	1. a person-at-arms in service of a feudal superior or a patron
		1. *especially* a person-at-arms trained in both cavalry and martial combat, often with an understanding of, if not practice in, the use of magic
		2. a bodyguard assigned to a specific individual (c. 2025-Jul-15)
		3. a lover who acts as their partner's bodyguard (c. 2025-Jul-15)
	2. a rank within the Adin military service, the lowest rank of field army officer without a commission, see also [_gurneim_](lexicon/g/gurneim)
	3. a political appointment as a diplomatic person-at-arms, serving to resolve disputes by formal sporting conflict
2. champion, hero
	1. a promoter or supporter of a person, ideal, or cause (c. 2025-Jul-15)
3. knight, cavalier, cavalry, chariot, horseman, rider, horse, springer, jumper, mounted warrior, donkey
	1. (gaming) either of two pieces belonging to each color in a set of chess-soldiers which makes a G-shaped move of two spaces in axis of the board, and one space in the other axis, including the ability to move over spaces which may be occupied (r. 2025-Jul-14)
4. king, twins
	1. (gaming) the result of reaching the final rank with a checkers piece, denoted either by flipping the checker-piece to the obverse, or by stacking two checkers-pieces together. (c. 2025-Jul-14)
