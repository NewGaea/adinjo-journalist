## Noun

### nonkelorn (plural _nonkelornu_)

1. mesa, plateau (cn. 2025-Sep-16)
	1. a flatland occurring on the side of a mountain
	2. a flattened or constructed flat space on the side of a mountain
	3. a section of an argument or narrative during which tension stops increasing, but is not decreasing
2. \[sexuality] edge, goon, withheld orgasm (cn. 2025-Sep-16)
	1. the borderline between stimulation and orgasm
