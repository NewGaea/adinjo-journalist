## 1. Noun

### _náytèl_ (plural _náytèlu_)

1. uncle, older male/man relative (especially consanguine)
	1. a man or male ancestor who is the sibling of one's parent
	2. a man or male relative of a prior generation who is a sibling to one's lineal ancestor of that generation
		1. [_çunáytèl_](lexicon/c/çunáytèl.md) "great-uncle"
		2. [_ènlanáytèl_](lexicon/e/ènlanáytèl.md) "uncle by marriage"
2. mister, sir
	1. polite title or form of address for a man, especially one with whom the speaker is unfamiliar
		1. [_anáytèl_](lexicon/a/anáytèl.md) "good sir (high respect)"
3. mentor, senior, upperclassman, {先輩|せんぱい}
	1. a senior of the same rank or class as oneself, especially such a person as one looks up to or admires, may also be used by younger adults when speaking to their former teachers
