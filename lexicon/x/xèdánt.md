## Noun

### xèdánt (plural _xèdántu_)

1. soup, stew
	1. a liquid food prepared using stock as a base and often containing solid chunks of cooked food
2. curry, gravy
	1. a dish prepared using the essence of its ingredients as the basis for a thick sauce, usually served over a staple such as a bread, porridge, or boiled grains
3. bubble tea
	1. a cold beverage served with small pieces of fruit or sweets in the drinking vessel, often also served with ice to keep the beverage cold
