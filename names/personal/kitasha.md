# Kitasha

**Kitasha** is a slightly common feminine personal name. It is derived from _kita_ "dog" and _asha_ "peace," meaning "at peace with dogs." It is a particularly common name in rural environments, especially among families who keep and raise dogs. It is fairly rare on the Neo Olympus.
