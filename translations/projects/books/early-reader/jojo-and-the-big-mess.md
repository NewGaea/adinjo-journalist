# JoJo and the Big Mess

This is a translation, page by page, of _JoJo and the Big Mess_ by Jane O'Connor.

## Conventions

Personal Names are phoneticized into Adinjo Journalist, not localized or translated.

- JoJo ${\rightarrow}$ **JOJO**
- Nancy ${\rightarrow}$ **NÁNSI**
- Freddy ${\rightarrow}$ **FRÈDI**
- Frenchy ${\rightarrow}$ **FRÈNCHI**

### 32 - He is not Mad Now.

#### He is not Mad Now.

```ngloss
\ex Ju, ji eldha'ha icha.
\gl ju [ʒu] [now] [now]
    ji [ʒi] [3SM] [he]
    èldha(r)-ha [ɛl.ða.ha] [anger-NEG] [anger-not]
    ic-ha [i.t͜ʃa] [COP.PRES-NEG] [isn't]
\ft Now, he is not angry.
```

**Translator's Note:** This use of the double negative communicates that there has been a change in the anger of *popoi* "daddy," reinforcing that he *was* angry but is *èldhaha* "not angry" anymore.
#### This is the Best Mess of All.

```ngloss
\ex Banjaluai lan ic'rindyurin.
\gl ba-an-jaluai [ban.ʒa.lu.ˈaj] [this-be-messy] [this-mess]
    lan [lan] [self] [itself]
    ic-rin-dyur-in [it͜s.ˈɾin.dʲu.ˌɾin] [COP.PRES-POSQUAL-SUPERLATIVE-INTENSE] [is-good-most-good]
\ft This mess is the very best.
```

This line uses a nonstandard intensifier "_rindyurin_" which repeats _rin_ (well, good) after the superlative _dyur_. This can, however, be interpreted as a young child playing with the language similar to the childish English construction "bestest."
