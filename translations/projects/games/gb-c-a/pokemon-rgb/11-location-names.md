# Appendix B: Location Names

## Towns

### Masara / Palette Town

> [!example] Kevicpar
> 
> **_Kevicpar_** is a name for slate, particularly smoothed slate suitable for marking with either chalk or coal. *Kevicpar* is a typical flooring material, with smaller plates of it used to practice both handwriting and illustration. This name conveys that Kevicpar is a starting point, a foundation, and that it is the place from which you will begin learning how to teach and raise Pokémon. As a town, it receives no special classifier label.

### Shion / Lavender Town

> [!example] Lavèndyur
> 
> **_Lavèndyur_** is a deep pun playing off the idea of a pale flower, spirits in residence (or a haunting), and going pale -- it is also the name of amethyst, a form of quartz often associated by the Adin with spirits (both metaphysical and alcoholic). The gem is often embedded into Adin headstones or memorial images.
> 
> At least some truth exists to this belief, as various forms of Quartz (when properly prepared for magic and magitech uses) are very capable of capturing and storing engrammatic patterns and retaining "memory" in stable patterns for long times.

## Cities

### Tokiwa / Viridian City

> [!example] Kangre Naberon
> 
> **_Naberon_** is the name of chromia, or chromium (iii) oxide, or any type of stone with high levels of chromium oxide. This compound produces a rich deep green shade, and can even be processed to produce a pigment of this color. As a color, it is associated with evergreen trees, mossy forests, and new growth.

### Nibi / Pewter City

> [!example] Kangre Novroc
> 
> **_Novroc_** is a generic term for igneous mountain stone, and may refer to mica, light granite, or andesite. This sort of stone is almost always of a gray (_fir_) shade, though it may range outside of this norm into somewhat bluish or ruddy hues, though it typically retains a low saturation. Novroc is commonly used for cobblestone, stone bricks, and making clay for construction purposes (such as roofing or mosaic tiles).

### Hanada / Cerulean City

> [!example] Kangre Arzolwán
> 
> **_Arzolwán_** is the name for lapis lazuli, especially as used for decorative purposes and the basis of ultramarine pigment. Long held to be closely related to elemental water and as an opaque version of the sapphire (_dwán_), this color is associated with vast waters and skies, and is seen a one of the most valuable minerals by jewelers, artists, artificers, alchemists, and magic users.
