### Meet the Command Crew

CHEYRIN: Dandin DARMI, lo aur delman mokuretaxu dama natha hemlétot?

DARMI: Daiti, kushi romurén, xi ju hemlé folrens.

CHEYRIN: Tech, yi hemlé Dandin.

### Panic! In the Shuttle

BÉBÍ: Kufi sinju yi lamatot'dul sugo!

ARÌK: Folres, folres; ka folryakun, fi ci jimartolochi…

BÉBÍ: Aur fi kempomer? Lo, ki romurén nikar ic.

ARÌK: Shi fi jimarmer ayuga yanitol!

### In the Captain's Name

CHEYRIN: Aur sif shi bitke kempons… Dandin, shi natha hemlé romurén taktochi! Ju!

DARMI: Mokuretaxu, kuci bitke i romurén biderochi ju. Ba demargel ic, Saukentax CHEYRIN inda.

  

ARÌK: Cholrex! Kuyi jia larnirin lamo!

BÉBÍ: Kinar, kinar…

ARÌK: Ki… cilarn demargel i Saukentax kannutocha. Ji Dandin ic, sug Saukentax hort! Kuci sinju, lorn kasykun!

BÉBÍ: Aa! Tech, yi kempykun'rin. Yi fi Saukentax ankoro ijintota!

### The Doors Open

Bitkenu i romurén biderol.

ARÌK maron'dun, BÉBÍ kérin reczol.

KuBÉBÍ sinju lorn ic, fi yanitona.

DARMI hi CHEYRIN shi larn moxuretaxu zenton, kushi sinjunu lorn ic jua.

### Interrogation

CHEYRIN: Mokuretaxu, reczochi!

ARÌK hi BÉBÍ reczon'dul ju.

CHEYRIN: Yi ci hemlé anpera icha. Sif aur cinu bitkenu cidjot? Hi, ira larn ci kempons, hi ira ci kempyans?

### The Captain's Patience

Tok felimin dapicon.

Larn mokuretaxu Saukentax hi Dandin kérin reczol, dekcin yanitona.

CHEYRIN felimin folres.

CHEYRIN: Mokure ARÌK, aur ci kérinjia hemlétot, techi?

ARÌK: Te- Techi, Saukentax.

CHEYRIN: Ma… aur ci k'áye yanis'dun jimartolot, techi. Aur yi ayuga jimartol, techi?

ARÌK: Te- Techi, Saukentax.

CHEYRIN: Hi ci… Ci aur Mokure BÉBÍ, techi?

BÉBÍ: Techi… a, techi Saukentax.

CHEYRIN: Hi BÉBÍ, aur monsi ci hemlétot?

BÉBÍ: Aa, sa… Bajia, Saukentax.

CHEYRIN: Dandin, bajia kufi jia winirin promeitochi. Jua, bajia kuji jia larinirin promeitochi.

DARMI: Techi, Saukentax. Promeitot.

### Reviewing the Situation

CHEYRIN: Dandin, monsi ki natha hemlétot, aur bitkenu anbider, techi?

DARMI: Techi, Saukentax.

CHEYRIN: Te... Hi, aur del nanda mokuretaxu romurén nikar ins?

DARMI: Larn, Saukentax. Banu larn mokuretaxu, lamo.

CHEYRIN: Aa, aur lamo? Aur banu mokuretaxu lamo? Ma, win, yr larnau, shilarn kempons. 

NARRATOR: Saukentax larn mokuretaxu takem baciton.

CHEYRIN: Mokure BÉBÍ, yanitochi! Aur ci bitkenu cidjot? Xau!

### The Confession

BÉBÍ: Aa, ma... xau nikar...

ARÌK: Saukentax! Yi kinar ic, yi kempons! Mokure BÉBÍ atakama kempyans!

BÉBÍ: Aur? Saukentax! Aba kuyi midox ic! Yi bitkenu cidjot! Ji atakama kempyans!

CHEYRIN: Ma, ci larnau 'yi kempons' yaniton. Kend, yi aur Mokure BÉBÍ aurot, techi?

### Punishment

CHEYRIN: Dandin, k'yi dekchinu promeitochi. Mokurenu ARÌK hi BÉBÍ larnau midox jun ic. Shi'lan aurok cidjochi, hi djoi kolbu kruchitocha. Techi.

ARÌK: Aurok cidjochi!?

BÉBÍ: Djoi'ha!?

CHEYRIN: Hi, gojia, cilarn yi hemlétochi. Cilarn sug cholauroku kuretochi.

ARÌK: Te-techi, Saukentax.

BÉBÍ: Techi... Saukentax...

DARMI: Promeitot, Saukentax.
