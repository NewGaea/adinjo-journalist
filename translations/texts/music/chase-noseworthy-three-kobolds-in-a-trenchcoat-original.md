# _Three Kobolds in a Trenchcoat_ (Original) (Chase Noseworthy)

## English Lyrics

### Verse 1

Late one night before the tavernkeeper shut his door
A wobbly gentleman came in, his footing was unsure
Moustachioed and handsome, and his clothing touched the floor
He was no man at all, he was
Three kobolds in a trenchcoat

### Chorus

One's on the bottom, strong is he
Two's in the middle, carrying Three
Three's pretending not to be
Three kobolds in a trenchcoat

### Verse 2

One day when a maiden was out on her nightly stroll
She got lost in the forest and was set on by a gnoll
When in there stepped a gentleman, moustachioed and tall
He was no help at all 'cause he's
Three kobolds in a trenchcoat

\[Chorus]

### Verse 3

Once there was a king who was a scourge upon his land
The citizens all wished him dead, his rule they couldn't stand
One day day he fell dead on the pavement by no hand
He happened to have died next to
Three kobolds in a trenchcoat

\[Chorus]

### Verse 4

The people of the kingdom, the man's praises they did sing
Believing that he was the one who slew their awful king
Put on his head a crown, and to the throne they did him bring
But little did they know he was
Three kobolds in a trenchcoat

\[Chorus]

### Verse 5

I didn't take them long to hate their brand new overlord
He butchered up the tax code and put rebels to the sword
From out of his own kingdom he was driven by the horde
The economy had been ruined by
Three kobolds in a trenchcoat

\[Chorus]

### Verse 6

And if you're wondering how I sing three part harmony
When I am just one man without another to be seen

Three kobolds in a trenchcoat
