# Welcome to the Show / Music in My Heart

This is a translation of a song from _Equestria Girls: Rainbow Rocks_, a film related to the _My Little Pony_ franchise. Character and Group names are not translated, only roughly transliterated (excluding articles). The title of the song in Adinjo is:

```ngloss
\ex Cabindak Chérìlyas / Porpungal i Kuyi Lav
\ft Greetings from the Show / Music of My Soul
```

## Verse \[The Dazzlings]

### Welcome to the Show

```ngloss
\ex Cabindak chérìlyas.
\gl cabind-tak [t͜sa.ˈbin.dak] [show.GER-ABL] [showing-from]
    chérìlyas [ˈt͜ʃeː.ɾɪ.ˌlʲas] [introduce.VOC] [welcome]
\ft Greetings from the Show.
```

> [!Translation Note]
> In Adinjo, greetings like this come _from_ something, and so use the positional _tak._ This is a contrast to the English where one is welcomed _to_ a place, group, etc. Note that if not specified, the greeting is from the speaker or a dummy subject.

### We're here to Let You Know (ah-ah-ah-ah) Our time is now (ah-ah-ah-ah), Your time is Running out (ah-ah-ah)

```ngloss
\ex Ki ci ijin hachton: Kuki deirin carmton, cin ki'hèm dantoch cir...
\gl ki [ki] [1.PL] [we]
    ci [t͜si] [2.SG]
    ijin [i.ˈʒin] [know.INF] [to know]
    hach-ton [hat͜ʃ.ton] [wish-PRES] [wish]
    ku-ki [ku.ˈkiː] [GEN-1.PL] [our]
    deirin [ˈdej.ɾin] [power] [power]
    carmi-ton [t͜saɹm.ton] [grow-PRES] [grows]
    ci-nu [t͜sin] [2.PL] [you all]
    ki-hemlé [ki.hɛm] [1.PL-towards] [to us]
    dan-tochi [dan.tot͜ʃ] [serve.FUT] [will serve]
    cir [t͜sir] [PROX.FUT] [soon]
\ft We wish for you to know: Our power grows, you will soon serve us
```

> [!Translation Note]
> As a poetic verse, a variety of elisions are used to help maintain the meter here, particularly dropping vowels that would otherwise add extra feet to the lyrics. Most notable here are the dropping of /i/ from _carmiton_ "grows" and _dantochi_ "will serve", as well as the /u/ from _cinu_ "you all."
>
> While this form of elision is common in poetry and high-paced speech, it is discouraged in formal speech.
