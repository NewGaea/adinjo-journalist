# _I Saw Mommy Kissing Santa Claus_ (Holiday, Tommie Connor)

## Verse

### I Saw Mommy Kissing Santa Claus, underneath the Mistletoe Last Night.

```ngloss
\ex Kyococi yi memé hi SANTA KLAUS misàltó blut shyulen bacitot.
\ft Last night, I saw mommy and Santa Claus kissing under the mistletoe.
```

### She Didn't Hear Me Creep down the Stairs to Have a Peep:

```ngloss
\ex Fi k'yi bacireinda felimarin jimartota:
\ft She heardn't my sneaking for peeking:
```

### She Thought that I Was Tucked up in My Bedroom Fast Asleep.

```ngloss
\ex Yi fainaurok ni fainutoldyur fi minicolot.
\ft She thought me deep asleep in my bedroom.
```

## Chorus

### Then I Saw Mommy Tickle Santa Claus, underneath His Beard so Snowy White.

```ngloss
\ex Kyorel, memé SANTA KLAUS hemlé, kuji bedji blut vic toshi qimalder.
\ft Just then, mommy reached out to Santa Claus, below his snow-white beard she stroked her hand.
```

### Oh, what a Laugh it Would Have Been if Daddy Had only Seen Mommy Kissing Santa Claus Last Night.

```ngloss
\ex Ai! Sif popó ba bacolot, ju yi gexalatochi'mer, hi SANTA KLAUS tréno kintaton.
\ft Hey! If daddy had seen this, now I would laugh, and I'd feel sorry for Santas Claus.
```

#### Alternative Last Line

This variant may be used for a final repetition:

```ngloss
\ex Ai! Yi sug xai minicon, ya SANTA KLAUS hi memé, memé hi popó lin shyuletot.
\ft Hey! What I'm thinking is the truth, is that Santa Claus and mommy, were kissing like mommy and daddy.
```
