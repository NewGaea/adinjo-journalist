# _All Star_ (Smash Mouth, 1999)

This translation documents an attempt to handle colloquial expressions in a less literal translation and a more vibes and rhythm matching form.

## Verse I

### Somebody once told me the world is gonna roll me

```ngloss
\ex Xi yi'hem yanitot, sug muri yi jak'hautochi
\ft They said to me the world'll abandon me
```

### I ain't the sharpest tool in the shed

```ngloss
\ex Sug rinduldyur yi anitona,
\ft I'm not the very best or brightest.
```

### She was looking kind of dumb with her finger and her thumb / In the shape of an "L" on her forehead

```ngloss
\ex Xi'lan ayenu jèn sinju prame "XOI"dai lin xi'lan nij yi'hem tago, yi loika lin 
\ft Hands crossed like "X" before their face and say "Get out of this place," gonna exorcise me like I'm a demon.
```

## Bridge I

### Well the years start coming and they don't stop coming

```ngloss
\ex Nyorénu mariton, maritochi atemba
\ft Years walk forward, and they keep on walking
```

### Fed to the rules and I hit the ground running

```ngloss
\ex Alnomu hem jalutolot kend jamar caluton
\ft Bound to the rules and I hit the ground running
```

### Didn't make sense not to live for fun

```ngloss
\ex Delmaré inda jene kuyi luketot'rin
\ft Life for pleasure was my best choice
```
### Your brain gets smart but your head gets dumb

```ngloss
\ex Enros airetonrin, kend mori airetonkin
\ft Your brain is smart but your heart is dumb
```

### So much to do, so much to see / So what's wrong with taking the back streets?

```ngloss
\ex Akempodul, abacidul / Ma yi jak'dashinu hem ventrotoci
\ft All we can do, all we can see / So I want to travel the back roads.
```

### You'll never know if you don't go

```ngloss
\ex Sif hemlétona, chijitocha
\ft If you don't go, you won't know
```

### You'll never shine if you don't glow

```ngloss
\ex Sif burmetona, yi iptocha
\ft If you don't burn, you won't shine
```

## Chorus I

### Hey now, you're an all-star, get your game on, go play

```ngloss
\ex Éyo, kendin preda, ayginstolochi, shenzai! 
\ft Hey you, sports legend, play your game, win!
```
### Hey now, you're a rock star, get the show on, get paid

```ngloss
\ex Éyo, kendin shoxem, porpuntochi, daleru!
\ft Hey you, singing legend, play songs, get money!
```
### And all that glitters is gold

```ngloss
\ex Ayuga aniptan orind ich
\ft Everything glittering is gold
```
### Only shooting stars break the mold

```ngloss
\ex Din lamo wi ímat jia shenzatochi
\ft Only those who shoot can win the day.
```
