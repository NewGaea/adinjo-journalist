# 12 Levels of Sentence Complexity

Originally posted to Reddit on Sunday, 28 September 2025.

## Level 1 – First Day

_(Basic SVO / Noun + Verb)_

> I see a dog.

1. **Yi kita baciton.** >> "I dog see."

```ngloss
\ex Yi kita baciton.
\ft I see a dog
```

## Level 2 – Easy

_(Present Continuous / Motion)_

> You are walking to the market.

2. **Ci agro'hemlé marton.** >> "You marketward walk."

```ngloss
\ex Ci agro'hemlé marton
\ft You walk to the market.
```

## Level 3 – Under-Average

_(Adverbial / Habitual Aspect)_

> They often eat together after work.

3. **Coritem, shi dobraun goju kruchiton varmel.** >> "Sometime, they work-after eat together."

```ngloss
\ex Coritem, shi dobraun goju kruchiton varmel.
\ft Sometime, they eat together after working.
```

## Level 4 – Average

_(Future / Subordinate Clause)_

> We will meet again when the sun rises.

4. **Monsi kuha xajiton, ki'lan dangetochi janyu.** >> "When the sun is rising, we-self will meet again."

```ngloss
\ex Monsi kuha xajiton, ki'lan dangetochi janyu.
\ft When the sun is rising, we shall meet again.
```

## Level 5 – Above-Average

_(Relative Clause / Possession)_

> The man who lives next door gave me a strange gift.

5. **Sug nen, domya'tak kyorel, yi'hemlé banchi taksu bancot.** >> "The man, from-house next, to-me a gift strange gave."

```ngloss
\ex Sug nen, domya'tak kyorel, yi'hemlé banchi taksu bancot.
\ft The man, from the next house, gave me a strange gift.
```

## Level 6 – Hard

_(Volition / Subordination / Negation)_

> I wanted to help her, but she refused to listen.

6. **Yi fi'hemlé chánt kiréntot, kentin fi jimar kannutoa.** >> "I to-her to help wanted, but she to listen refused."

```ngloss
\ex Yi fi'hemlé chánt kiréntot, kentin fi jimar kannutoa.
\ft I wished to assist her, however she refused to hear me out.
```

## Level 7 – Very Hard

_(Perfect Aspect / Concession)_

> Even though the storm had ended, the streets were still flooded.

7. **Gubra stram birot, adashinu anbeváxyemn ins.** >> "Although the storm was finished, all paths be-flood were."

```ngloss
\ex Gubra stram birot, adashinu anbeváxyemn ins.
\ft Although the storm ended, all the paths were flooded.
```

## Level 8 – Complex Grammar

_(Conditional Perfect / Modality)_

> If I had spoken more carefully, they might have understood my meaning.

8. **Sif yi yanitot lofar'dul, shi kuyi mors injintot'galm.** >> "If I spoke more slowly, they my purpose might-understood."

```ngloss
\ex Sif yi yanitot lofar'dul, shi kuyi mors injintot'galm.
\ft If I spoke slower, they may have understood my purpose.
```

> [!Note] Permissive Mood
> This translation uses the permissive mood, suggesting that the inability of "them" to understand was actually deliberate on the part of "me." It could also be translated as, "If I had spoken slower, I might have permitted them to understand my purpose."
> 
> To place more of the burden on the listeners, the permissive suffix _-galm_ could be replaced with _-mer_, suggesting the difficulty in understanding was in the ability of the listener, not a matter of permission or the speaker's intent.

## Level 9 – Advanced Syntax

_(Embedded Clause / Reported Speech)_

> The book that you said your sister wanted to borrow has already been returned.

9. **Sug twa, ira yi kuyi coté bor yanitot, ki narikas baju.** >> "The book, which you your sister to borrow said, we hold right now."

```ngloss
\ex Sug twa, ira yi kuyi coté bor yanitot, ki narikas baju.
\ft The book, which you said your sister wanted to acquire, we now have it in stock.
```

## Level 10 – Hypothetical & Modal

_(Conditional Mood / Necessity)_

> Should they decide to leave before dawn, we will have no choice but to follow.

10. **Sif xi asént kérin dép luxetoçi, ki dash aluxesha chumatocha.** >> "If they dawn before to depart choose, we to follow absolute-no-choice will have not."

```ngloss
\ex Sif xi asént kérin dép luxetoçi, ki dash aluxesha chumatocha.
\ft If they choose to depart before dawn, we'll not have any choice but to follow.
```

## Level 11 – Abstract & Philosophical

_(Metaphor / Abstract Nouns)_

> Knowledge without wisdom is like a tree without roots: it grows, but it cannot stand.

11. **Chijin sèda horta lin xagi sèdanu lin, xi acoréton'mer kentin rezton'mera.** >> "Information foundation without like tree roots without, it grows able however stands able not."

```ngloss
\ex Chijin sèda horta lin xagi sèdanu lin, xi acoréton'mer kentin rezton'mera.
\ft Data without context is like a tree without roots, it can spread out but not stand tall.
```

## Level 12 – Master Level

_(Complex Sentence / Multiple Clauses / Abstract Concept)_

> Despite centuries of conflict and countless broken promises, humanity continues its relentless pursuit of harmony, as if driven by an unquenchable longing for something it has never truly known.

12. **Gubra poxenu i kamanu hi ganwasu anpol anandaha, la kuGAEA dinu asha anejis afolryas, ma kiréngyu anmarxa coriyugi chijina xau atemba inda sedratol.**  >> "Despite ages of wars and promises broken innumerable, yet GAEA's people peace are pursuing absolutely stopless, by wishing utterly undying something unknown truly forever for driven."

```ngloss
\ex Gubra poxenu i kamanu hi ganwasu anpol anandaha, la kuGAEA dinu asha anejis afolryas, ma kiréngyu anmarxa coriyugi chijina xau atemba inda sedratol.
\ft Despite ages of wars and innumerable broken promises, the people of Gaea yet pursue peace endlessly, driven by undying wishes for something truly and ever unknown.
```
